# A2 — Part 2 (Units 6-10)

## Tema: No como carne {#nivel=A2 #unidad=6 #sujeto=comida-recetas}

### Gramática

#### G · Direct object pronouns (lo/la/los/las) {#cat=pronombres}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** The direct object pronouns **lo, la, los, las** replace a direct object (the "what" or "whom" of the verb) once it is already clear from the context, so you don't have to repeat it. They agree in gender and number with the noun they replace. They go **before** a conjugated verb, but attach to the end of an infinitive, a gerund, or an affirmative command. **Lo** can also be neuter, replacing a whole idea (*esto, eso, aquello, algo*).

**Examples.**
- ¿Dónde está la miel? — **La** he guardado en el armario. — *Where is the honey? — I've put it in the cupboard.*
- ¿Has comprado los tomates? — Sí, **los** compré ayer. — *Did you buy the tomatoes? — Yes, I bought them yesterday.*
- Este pan está buenísimo. ¿Dónde **lo** has comprado? — *This bread is delicious. Where did you buy it?*
- Voy a preparar la ensalada. Voy a preparar**la** ahora. — *I'm going to make the salad. I'm going to make it now.*
- ¿Sabes que abren un supermercado nuevo? — Sí, **lo** sé. — *Do you know they're opening a new supermarket? — Yes, I know (it).*

**Table.**

| Replaces | Pronoun | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| masc. singular (el queso) | lo | **Lo** corto en trozos. | I cut it into pieces. |
| fem. singular (la fruta) | la | **La** lavo bien. | I wash it well. |
| masc. plural (los huevos) | los | **Los** bato. | I beat them. |
| fem. plural (las patatas) | las | **Las** frío en aceite. | I fry them in oil. |
| a whole idea / "it" (neuter) | lo | No **lo** sé. | I don't know (it). |

**Ojo.** Don't use a direct object pronoun when the object has no determiner (article, possessive, demonstrative): *¿Esta tortilla lleva cebolla? — No, no lleva.* (not *no la lleva*). Also, don't confuse direct object **lo/la** with indirect object **le** (to him/her).

#### G · Impersonal se (se + 3rd person) {#cat=pronombres}

**Rule.** When we don't want to say (or don't know) who does the action — typically in recipes, instructions and generalizations — we use **se + the third person** of the verb. If the thing acted on is **singular**, the verb is third-person **singular**; if it is **plural**, the verb is third-person **plural**. English usually translates this with "you", "one", "they" or the passive.

**Examples.**
- Primero **se pelan** las patatas y **se cortan** en trozos. — *First you peel the potatoes and cut them into pieces.*
- En este restaurante **se come** muy bien. — *You eat really well in this restaurant.*
- El gazpacho **se sirve** frío. — *Gazpacho is served cold.*
- En España **se cena** muy tarde. — *In Spain people have dinner very late.*
- **Se baten** los huevos y luego **se echa** un poco de sal. — *You beat the eggs and then add a little salt.*

**Table.**

| Subject acted on | Structure | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| singular noun | se + 3rd person **singular** | Se calienta el aceite. | You heat the oil. |
| plural noun | se + 3rd person **plural** | Se calientan las patatas. | You heat the potatoes. |
| no explicit noun (general) | se + 3rd person **singular** | Aquí se come muy bien. | You eat well here. |
| generic "you" (informal) | tú-form, 2nd sing. | Pones aceite y lo calientas. | You put oil and heat it. |

**Ojo.** The verb agrees with the food/thing, not with a person: *se **pelan** las frutas* (plural), *se **pela** la fruta* (singular). A common error is leaving the verb singular with a plural noun.

#### G · Ser vs Estar {#cat=ser-estar-hay}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** To **describe** something or give a general **evaluation** of it (a quality it usually has), use **ser**. But to comment on a **direct, present experience** — especially how food tastes right now — use **estar**. So *este queso es bueno* means "this cheese is (generally) good", while *¡qué bueno está este queso!* means "this cheese tastes great (as I eat it now)".

**Examples.**
- Los quesos manchegos **son** excelentes. — *Manchego cheeses are excellent.* (general quality)
- ¡Qué buena **está** esta sopa! — *This soup tastes great!* (tasting it now)
- El restaurante **es** muy bonito, pero la comida **está** fría. — *The restaurant is very nice, but the food is cold.*
- Esta fruta **es** muy sana. — *This fruit is very healthy.* (a fact)
- Las manzanas todavía no **están** maduras. — *The apples aren't ripe yet.* (current state)

**Table.**

| Use | Verb | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| description / general quality | ser | El jamón ibérico **es** delicioso. | Iberian ham is delicious. |
| classification / what it is | ser | El gazpacho **es** una sopa fría. | Gazpacho is a cold soup. |
| direct, present experience (taste) | estar | ¡Qué rico **está** el gazpacho hoy! | The gazpacho tastes great today! |
| current, changeable state | estar | El pan **está** un poco duro. | The bread is a bit hard. |

**Ojo.** With food, *ser bueno* = "is good for you / good quality", but *estar bueno* = "tastes good" (and, about a person, can mean "is attractive" — use it carefully!).

#### G · Connectors (y, pero, además) {#cat=interrogativos-conectores}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** Connectors join ideas and show the logical relation between them. **Y** simply adds a second element. **Pero** adds an element that contrasts with the first. **Además** adds an element that **reinforces** the first idea ("what's more, besides").

**Examples.**
- Es un restaurante bonito **y** moderno. — *It's a nice and modern restaurant.*
- La sopa está buena, **pero** le falta sal. — *The soup is good, but it needs salt.*
- Estas galletas están muy buenas **y**, **además**, son muy ligeras. — *These biscuits are very good and, what's more, very light.*
- Me encanta el café, **pero** el médico me lo ha prohibido. — *I love coffee, but the doctor has forbidden it.*
- El supermercado es barato y, **además**, está abierto hasta muy tarde. — *The supermarket is cheap and, besides, it's open until very late.*

**Table.**

| Connector | Function | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| y | adds (neutral) | Compro pan y leche. | I buy bread and milk. |
| pero | adds a contrast | Es caro, pero muy bueno. | It's expensive, but very good. |
| además | adds and reinforces | Es sano y, además, barato. | It's healthy and, besides, cheap. |

**Ojo.** **Y** becomes **e** before a word starting with the *i-/hi-* sound: *jamón **e** higos* (ham and figs). Don't confuse *pero* (but) with *porque* (because).

### En contexto

**Diálogo — Preparando la cena de la clase**
> **Marta:** ¿Qué preparamos para la cena de la clase?
> **Diego:** Podemos hacer una tortilla de patata. Es fácil y a todos les gusta.
> **Marta:** Vale. ¿Sabes cómo se hace?
> **Diego:** Claro. Primero se pelan las patatas y se cortan en trozos pequeños.
> **Marta:** ¿Y luego?
> **Diego:** Luego se fríen en aceite. Después se baten los huevos y se mezcla todo.
> **Marta:** ¿Le ponemos cebolla?
> **Diego:** Yo la pongo siempre, pero mucha gente no la quiere.
> **Marta:** Además de la tortilla, ¿hacemos una ensalada?
> **Diego:** Sí, pero la preparamos al final, ¿vale?

*Translation:* Marta: What shall we make for the class dinner? — Diego: We can make a potato omelette. It's easy and everyone likes it. — Marta: OK. Do you know how it's made? — Diego: Of course. First you peel the potatoes and cut them into small pieces. — Marta: And then? — Diego: Then you fry them in oil. Afterwards you beat the eggs and mix everything. — Marta: Shall we put onion in it? — Diego: I always put it in, but a lot of people don't want it. — Marta: Besides the omelette, shall we make a salad? — Diego: Yes, but we'll make it at the end, OK?

**More examples:**
- En mi país, el pescado se come mucho, pero la carne no tanto. — *In my country, fish is eaten a lot, but not so much meat.*
- ¿Has probado el gazpacho? — Sí, lo probé ayer y estaba buenísimo. — *Have you tried gazpacho? — Yes, I tried it yesterday and it was delicious.*
- Para hacer una buena ensalada hay que usar tomates bien maduros. — *To make a good salad you have to use nice ripe tomatoes.*
- El vino de esta región es excelente y, además, no es muy caro. — *The wine from this region is excellent and, besides, it's not very expensive.*
- Compro las legumbres a peso porque es más barato. — *I buy pulses by weight because it's cheaper.*
- ¿Dónde has puesto el queso? — Lo he guardado en el frigorífico. — *Where did you put the cheese? — I've kept it in the fridge.*
- No como productos de origen animal: soy vegano. — *I don't eat animal products: I'm vegan.*

**Truco.** Remember: with food, **ser** describes what it is like in general (*es sano* = it's healthy), but **estar** reacts to it right now (*¡está riquísimo!* = it tastes amazing). And for the impersonal **se**, ask "how many things?" — one thing → *se pela*, many things → *se pelan*.

## Tema: Nos gustó mucho {#nivel=A2 #unidad=7 #sujeto=experiencias-lugares}

### Gramática

#### G · Pretérito perfecto {#cat=pasado}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** The pretérito perfecto (*he + past participle*) talks about the past connected to the present. Use it (1) to ask/say **whether** an action has happened, without caring when (*alguna vez, nunca, todavía no, ya*), and (2) for actions in a time period that includes now (*hoy, esta semana, este año*). Form it with the present of **haber** + the **past participle** (‑ar → ‑ado; ‑er/‑ir → ‑ido).

**Examples.**
- ¿**Has estado** alguna vez en México? — *Have you ever been to Mexico?*
- Nunca **he probado** la comida vasca. — *I've never tried Basque food.*
- Esta semana **hemos visitado** tres museos. — *This week we've visited three museums.*
- Todavía no **he visto** esa película. — *I haven't seen that film yet.*
- ¿Ya **habéis comido**? — *Have you eaten already?*

**Table.**

| Person | haber | + participle (visitar) | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | he | visitado | I have visited |
| tú | has | visitado | you have visited |
| él/ella/usted | ha | visitado | he/she has, you have visited |
| nosotros/as | hemos | visitado | we have visited |
| vosotros/as | habéis | visitado | you (pl.) have visited |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | han | visitado | they/you have visited |

**Ojo.** Some participles are irregular: *hacer → hecho, ver → visto, escribir → escrito, decir → dicho, poner → puesto, volver → vuelto, abrir → abierto, romper → roto, morir → muerto*. **Haber** and the participle never separate: *he visto* (not *he lo visto* — the pronoun goes before *he*: *lo he visto*).

#### G · Pretérito indefinido {#cat=pasado}

**Rule.** The pretérito indefinido reports a completed past action seen as **finished and disconnected from the present**. Use it with time markers like *ayer, el año pasado, el otro día, la semana pasada, en 2019, hace dos años*. Regular endings differ for ‑ar vs ‑er/‑ir.

**Examples.**
- Ayer **cené** en un restaurante vasco. — *Yesterday I had dinner in a Basque restaurant.*
- El año pasado **viajamos** a Costa Rica. — *Last year we travelled to Costa Rica.*
- **Vi** ballenas en la costa del Pacífico. — *I saw whales on the Pacific coast.*
- ¿Qué tal la excursión? — Me **gustó** mucho. — *How was the trip? — I liked it a lot.*
- El museo **abrió** en 1928. — *The museum opened in 1928.*

**Table.**

| Person | ‑ar (viajar) | ‑er (comer) | ‑ir (vivir) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | viajé | comí | viví |
| tú | viajaste | comiste | viviste |
| él/ella/usted | viajó | comió | vivió |
| nosotros/as | viajamos | comimos | vivimos |
| vosotros/as | viajasteis | comisteis | vivisteis |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | viajaron | comieron | vivieron |

**Ojo.** The choice is about **connection to now**, not distance in time: *este año he ido* (this year — includes now) vs *el año pasado fui* (last year — closed). Note the accents mark meaning: *viajo* (I travel, present) vs *viajó* (he travelled, past).

#### G · me/te/le… gustaría + infinitive {#cat=gustar-similares}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** To express a **wish or desire** to do something ("I would like to…"), use an indirect object pronoun (*me, te, le, nos, os, les*) + **gustaría** + an **infinitive**. Because it is followed by an infinitive, *gustaría* stays the same for every person. *Encantaría* works the same way, but stronger.

**Examples.**
- A mí me **gustaría** ir a la costa del Pacífico. — *I'd like to go to the Pacific coast.*
- ¿Te **gustaría** visitar el acuario? — *Would you like to visit the aquarium?*
- A ella le **encantaría** probar la cocina vasca. — *She'd love to try Basque cuisine.*
- Nos **gustaría** ver los templos de Bangkok. — *We'd like to see the temples in Bangkok.*
- A mis amigos les **gustaría** hacer una ruta en elefante. — *My friends would like to do an elephant trek.*

**Table.**

| Indirect pronoun | Person | + gustaría + inf. | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| me | (a mí) | me gustaría viajar | I would like to travel |
| te | (a ti) | te gustaría viajar | you would like to travel |
| le | (a él/ella/usted) | le gustaría viajar | he/she/you would like to travel |
| nos | (a nosotros/as) | nos gustaría viajar | we would like to travel |
| os | (a vosotros/as) | os gustaría viajar | you (pl.) would like to travel |
| les | (a ellos/ellas/ustedes) | les gustaría viajar | they/you would like to travel |

**Ojo.** Keep *gustaría* singular with an infinitive, even for plural ideas: *me gustaría ver los museos* (not *me gustarían ver*). The optional *a mí / a ti…* just adds emphasis or clarity.

#### G · Exclamative sentences (¡Qué…!) {#cat=interrogativos-conectores}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** Spanish exclamations of admiration or surprise are built with **¡Qué…!**. Use *¡Qué + adjective!* to react to a quality, *¡Qué + noun!* to react to a thing, and *¡Qué + noun + tan/más + adjective!* to intensify ("what a … !").

**Examples.**
- ¡**Qué** bonito! — *How lovely!*
- ¡**Qué** maravilla! — *How wonderful! / What a marvel!*
- ¡**Qué** lugar **tan** increíble! — *What an incredible place!*
- ¡**Qué** película **más** buena! — *What a great film!*
- ¡**Qué** envidia me das! — *I'm so jealous! (lit. what envy you give me)*

**Table.**

| Structure | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| ¡Qué + adjective! | ¡Qué caro! | How expensive! |
| ¡Qué + noun! | ¡Qué horror! | How awful! |
| ¡Qué + noun + tan + adj.! | ¡Qué ciudad tan bonita! | What a beautiful city! |
| ¡Qué + noun + más + adj.! | ¡Qué día más estupendo! | What a great day! |

**Ojo.** *Tan* and *más* are interchangeable here and both mean "so/such". Don't add an article: say *¡Qué día tan bonito!*, never *¡Qué un día…!*.

#### G · Verbs like gustar: parecer, caer bien/mal, pasárselo bien/mal {#cat=gustar-similares}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** Several verbs of opinion and feeling work like **gustar**: the person is an indirect object (*me, te, le, nos, os, les*) and the verb agrees with the **thing/person** being judged. **Parecer** = "to seem/find" (+ adjective). **Caer bien/mal** = to like/dislike a person's character. **Pasárselo bien/mal** = to have a good/bad time.

**Examples.**
- ¿Qué te **pareció** la película? — Me **pareció** buenísima. — *What did you think of the film? — I found it great.*
- Los libros me **parecieron** un poco aburridos. — *The books seemed a bit boring to me.*
- Conocí a sus padres y me **cayeron** muy bien. — *I met his parents and I really liked them.*
- Ese chico no me **cae** bien. — *I don't like that guy (his character).*
- En la fiesta me **lo pasé** fenomenal. — *I had a great time at the party.*

**Table.**

| Verb | + one thing (sing.) | + several things (pl.) | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| parecer | me pareció | me parecieron | I found it / them |
| caer bien | me cayó bien | me cayeron bien | I liked (person/people) |
| caer mal | me cayó mal | me cayeron mal | I disliked (person/people) |
| pasárselo (bien) | me lo pasé bien | — (fixed expression) | I had a good time |

**Table.**

| Person | pasárselo (indefinido) | English |
|---|---|---|
| yo | me lo pasé | I had a … time |
| tú | te lo pasaste | you had a … time |
| él/ella/usted | se lo pasó | he/she/you had a … time |
| nosotros/as | nos lo pasamos | we had a … time |
| vosotros/as | os lo pasasteis | you (pl.) had a … time |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | se lo pasaron | they/you had a … time |

**Ojo.** With *un poco* + adjective, we imply a **negative** trait: *me pareció un poco caro* (a bit expensive = criticism). Don't say *un poco* with positive adjectives. Also note *caer bien* is about personality, while *gustar* about a person can sound romantic.

### En contexto

**Diálogo — Hablando de un viaje**
> **Lucía:** ¿Has estado alguna vez en Costa Rica?
> **Pablo:** No, no he estado nunca. ¿Y tú?
> **Lucía:** Sí, estuve el año pasado y me encantó.
> **Pablo:** ¿Qué tal? ¿Qué te pareció?
> **Lucía:** ¡Increíble! Vi ballenas en la costa del Pacífico. ¡Qué lugar tan bonito!
> **Pablo:** ¡Qué envidia! A mí me gustaría ir a la selva.
> **Lucía:** La gente también me cayó muy bien, es muy simpática.
> **Pablo:** ¿Y la comida?
> **Lucía:** Buenísima. Me lo pasé fenomenal.

*Translation:* Lucía: Have you ever been to Costa Rica? — Pablo: No, I've never been. And you? — Lucía: Yes, I went last year and I loved it. — Pablo: How was it? What did you think of it? — Lucía: Incredible! I saw whales on the Pacific coast. What a beautiful place! — Pablo: I'm so jealous! I'd like to go to the jungle. — Lucía: I really liked the people too, they're very friendly. — Pablo: And the food? — Lucía: Delicious. I had a fantastic time.

**More examples:**
- ¿Qué te pareció el museo? — Me pareció fascinante. — *What did you think of the museum? — I found it fascinating.*
- Nunca he probado la cocina vasca, pero me gustaría. — *I've never tried Basque cuisine, but I'd like to.*
- El otro día fui a una exposición de cerámica y me encantó. — *The other day I went to a ceramics exhibition and I loved it.*
- Conocí a Marta ayer y me cayó genial. — *I met Marta yesterday and I really liked her.*
- ¡Qué ciudad tan espectacular! ¿Cómo se llama? — *What a spectacular city! What's it called?*
- Este año hemos visitado tres países diferentes. — *This year we've visited three different countries.*
- A nosotros nos gustaría hacer deportes de aventura en la selva. — *We'd like to do adventure sports in the jungle.*

**Truco.** To choose between the two past tenses, listen to the time word: *hoy, esta semana, alguna vez, todavía* → **pretérito perfecto** (*he visto*); *ayer, el año pasado, el otro día* → **pretérito indefinido** (*vi*).

## Tema: Estamos muy bien {#nivel=A2 #unidad=8 #sujeto=cuerpo-salud}

### Gramática

#### G · Ser vs Estar {#cat=ser-estar-hay}

> [NIVEL A2] extends the A1 rule.

**Rule.** Use **ser** to identify, define and give **permanent or objective** characteristics (who/what someone is, personality, profession, origin). Use **estar** for **states and moods**, **location/position**, and health. Some adjectives change meaning with each verb (*ser listo* = clever / *estar listo* = ready). Participles used as adjectives, and the adverbs *bien/mal*, always go with **estar**.

**Examples.**
- Carlos **es** muy tranquilo, pero hoy **está** nervioso. — *Carlos is very calm, but today he's nervous.*
- El médico **es** de Bilbao, pero **está** viviendo en Madrid. — *The doctor is from Bilbao, but he's living in Madrid.*
- La farmacia **está** cerca de aquí. — *The chemist's is near here.*
- **Estoy** un poco cansado y me **duele** la cabeza. — *I'm a bit tired and my head hurts.*
- Todo **está** bien, no te preocupes. — *Everything is fine, don't worry.*

**Table.**

| Use | Verb | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| identity / definition | ser | Es traductor. | He's a translator. |
| permanent characteristic | ser | Es muy responsable. | He's very responsible. |
| origin | ser | Somos italianos. | We're Italian. |
| mood / temporary state | estar | Está ansioso. | He's anxious. |
| location / position | estar | El teatro está cerca. | The theatre is nearby. |
| health / participle / bien-mal | estar | Está resfriado / muy bien. | He has a cold / is very well. |

**Ojo.** Watch the meaning-changing pairs: *ser aburrido* (to be boring) vs *estar aburrido* (to be bored); *ser rico* (to be rich / good quality) vs *estar rico* (to taste good). Feelings and health are **always** *estar*: *estoy triste*, *está enfermo* — never *soy triste*.

#### G · The verb doler {#cat=gustar-similares}

**Rule.** **Doler** ("to hurt") works like *gustar*: the body part is the grammatical **subject**, and the person who feels the pain is an **indirect object** (*me, te, le, nos, os, les*). Use **duele** with a singular body part and **duelen** with a plural one. It is *o→ue* stem-changing, but you'll mostly meet the third-person forms.

**Examples.**
- Me **duele** la cabeza. — *My head hurts.*
- ¿Te **duelen** los pies? — *Do your feet hurt?*
- A mi madre le **duele** el estómago. — *My mother's stomach hurts.*
- Nos **duelen** las piernas después de correr. — *Our legs hurt after running.*
- ¿Qué le **duele**, señora? — *What hurts, madam? / Where's the pain?*

**Table.**

| Indirect pronoun | + duele (singular part) | + duelen (plural parts) |
|---|---|---|
| me | me duele la espalda | me duelen los ojos |
| te | te duele la garganta | te duelen las muelas |
| le | le duele la cabeza | le duelen los pies |
| nos | nos duele el cuello | nos duelen los brazos |
| os | os duele el estómago | os duelen las rodillas |
| les | les duele el oído | les duelen los hombros |

**Ojo.** The verb agrees with the body part, not the person: *me duele**n** las piernas* (plural). To say the same thing another way, use *tener dolor de* + body part (*tengo dolor de cabeza*) — but there the noun stays singular and general.

#### G · The affirmative imperative {#cat=imperativo-condicional-gerundio}

**Rule.** The affirmative imperative gives advice, instructions and orders. It has four forms: **tú** and **vosotros/as** (informal), **usted** and **ustedes** (formal). The **tú** form is the present *tú* form minus the final ‑s (*comes → come*). The **vosotros** form replaces the infinitive's ‑r with ‑d (*comer → comed*). **Usted/ustedes** use the subjunctive endings. Pronouns attach to the end of the verb (*lávate, hidrátalos*).

**Examples.**
- **Bebe** mucha agua y **descansa**. — *Drink lots of water and rest.*
- **Tomad** esta medicina dos veces al día. — *Take this medicine twice a day.*
- **Póngase** crema protectora, señora. — *Put on some sun cream, madam.*
- **Haz** deporte tres veces a la semana. — *Do sport three times a week.*
- **Lávate** los pies e **hidrátalos** cada día. — *Wash your feet and moisturise them every day.*

**Table.**

| Person | comprar | comer | vivir |
|---|---|---|---|
| tú | compra | come | vive |
| usted | compre | coma | viva |
| vosotros/as | comprad | comed | vivid |
| ustedes | compren | coman | vivan |

**Table.** *(stem-changing verbs keep their change in tú/usted/ustedes)*

| Person | pensar (e→ie) | dormir (o→ue) | pedir (e→i) |
|---|---|---|---|
| tú | piensa | duerme | pide |
| usted | piense | duerma | pida |
| vosotros/as | pensad | dormid | pedid |
| ustedes | piensen | duerman | pidan |

**Table.** *(common irregular tú forms)*

| Verb | tú | usted | vosotros/as | ustedes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| decir | di | diga | decid | digan |
| hacer | haz | haga | haced | hagan |
| ir | ve | vaya | id | vayan |
| poner | pon | ponga | poned | pongan |
| salir | sal | salga | salid | salgan |
| ser | sé | sea | sed | sean |
| tener | ten | tenga | tened | tengan |
| venir | ven | venga | venid | vengan |

**Ojo.** The irregular ‑g forms (*pon, ten, ven, sal, haz, di*) only affect **tú**; the *usted/ustedes* forms follow the subjunctive (*ponga, tenga…*). With pronouns, an accent often appears to keep the stress: *lava → lávate*, *pon → ponte*.

## Tema: Antes y ahora {#nivel=A2 #unidad=9 #sujeto=pasado-historia}

### Gramática

#### G · Pretérito imperfecto {#cat=pasado}

**Rule.** The pretérito imperfecto describes **habits, routines, repeated actions and circumstances** in the past ("used to / was ‑ing / would"). It is like the present tense, but shifted to the past: it sets the scene rather than reporting a single finished event. Regular verbs take ‑aba (‑ar) or ‑ía (‑er/‑ir) endings; there are only **three** irregular verbs: *ser, ir, ver*.

**Examples.**
- Cuando **era** niño, **vivía** en un pueblo pequeño. — *When I was a child, I lived in a small village.*
- Antes la gente no **viajaba** mucho porque **era** caro. — *In the past people didn't travel much because it was expensive.*
- Todos los veranos **íbamos** a la playa. — *Every summer we used to go to the beach.*
- De joven, mi abuela **tenía** el pelo muy largo. — *As a young woman, my grandmother had very long hair.*
- **Eran** las ocho y **llovía**. — *It was eight o'clock and it was raining.*

**Table.** *(regular verbs)*

| Person | estar (‑ar) | tener (‑er) | vivir (‑ir) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | estaba | tenía | vivía |
| tú | estabas | tenías | vivías |
| él/ella/usted | estaba | tenía | vivía |
| nosotros/as | estábamos | teníamos | vivíamos |
| vosotros/as | estabais | teníais | vivíais |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | estaban | tenían | vivían |

**Table.** *(the three irregular verbs)*

| Person | ser | ir | ver |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | era | iba | veía |
| tú | eras | ibas | veías |
| él/ella/usted | era | iba | veía |
| nosotros/as | éramos | íbamos | veíamos |
| vosotros/as | erais | ibais | veíais |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | eran | iban | veían |

**Ojo.** All ‑er/‑ir imperfect endings carry an accent on the *í* (*tenía, vivíamos*), and the *nosotros* ‑ar form too (*estábamos, éramos, íbamos*). Use the imperfecto for description/habit, not for single completed events (that's the indefinido).

#### G · ya no / todavía {#cat=numeros-tiempo}

**Rule.** These two markers compare a past situation with the present. **Ya no** = "not any more": something that used to happen has **stopped**. **Todavía** (= *aún*) = "still": something from the past **continues**. Both are usually followed by a present-tense verb.

**Examples.**
- **Ya no** vivo en Madrid. — *I don't live in Madrid any more.*
- **Todavía** trabajo en el mismo sitio. — *I still work in the same place.*
- Ibiza **ya no** es una isla tranquila. — *Ibiza isn't a quiet island any more.*
- **Todavía** me gusta cultivar verduras en el jardín. — *I still like growing vegetables in the garden.*
- Mi abuelo **ya no** conduce, pero **todavía** monta en bici. — *My grandfather doesn't drive any more, but he still rides his bike.*

**Table.**

| Marker | Meaning | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| ya no | interruption (stopped) | Ya no fumo. | I don't smoke any more. |
| todavía / aún | continuity (still) | Todavía fumo. | I still smoke. |
| todavía no | not yet | Todavía no he terminado. | I haven't finished yet. |
| ya | already / now | Ya he terminado. | I've already finished. |

**Ojo.** *Ya no* + verb (stopped) is the opposite of *todavía* + verb (continues). Don't confuse *todavía* (still) with *todavía no* (not yet) — the little *no* flips the meaning.

#### G · Time markers for past and present {#cat=numeros-tiempo}

**Rule.** These expressions signal whether you're talking about the past or the present, which helps you pick the right tense. Past markers introduce habits/periods (with the imperfecto); "already mentioned period" markers refer back to a time you've spoken about; present markers point to now.

**Examples.**
- **De niño**, jugaba en la calle con mis amigos. — *As a child, I played in the street with my friends.*
- **A los 18 años** no sabía qué estudiar. — *At 18 I didn't know what to study.*
- **En aquella época** no teníamos televisión. — *At that time we didn't have a television.*
- **Hoy en día** viajar en avión es muy barato. — *Nowadays flying is very cheap.*
- **Actualmente** vivo en Sevilla. — *Currently I live in Seville.*

**Table.**

| Talking about the PAST | Talking about the PRESENT |
|---|---|
| antes (before / in the past) | ahora (now) |
| de niño/a, de joven (as a child/young person) | hoy en día (nowadays) |
| a los 15 años (at the age of 15) | actualmente (currently) |
| en aquella época, entonces (back then) | en estos momentos (right now) |
| en aquellos tiempos (in those days) | en la actualidad (at present) |

**Ojo.** *Entonces, en aquella época* and *en aquellos tiempos* refer back to a period you've **already mentioned**, so they usually pair with the imperfecto. *Antes* (before) contrasts the past with today.

## Tema: Momentos especiales {#nivel=A2 #unidad=10 #sujeto=anecdotas-emociones}

### Gramática

#### G · Irregular pretérito indefinido {#cat=pasado}

**Rule.** Many common verbs are irregular in the pretérito indefinido. Three groups: (1) **vowel-change** ‑ir verbs (*pedir, sentir, dormir*) change e→i or o→u **only** in the third persons; (2) **irregular-stem** verbs (*estar, tener, poder…*) use a special stem with one shared, unstressed set of endings; (3) **ser** and **ir**, which share the exact same irregular forms.

**Examples.**
- El camarero **pidió** la cuenta y todos **durmieron** bien. — *The waiter asked for the bill and everyone slept well.*
- Ayer **estuve** en casa de Carlos y **tuvimos** una cena estupenda. — *Yesterday I was at Carlos's and we had a great dinner.*
- No **pude** venir porque **hubo** un problema. — *I couldn't come because there was a problem.*
- **Hizo** mucho frío y nadie **quiso** salir. — *It was very cold and no one wanted to go out.*
- **Fui** al cine y luego **fui** muy feliz. — *(ir) I went to the cinema and then (ser) I was very happy.*

**Table.** *(vowel-change ‑ir verbs — only 3rd persons change)*

| Person | pedir (e→i) | sentir (e→i) | dormir (o→u) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | pedí | sentí | dormí |
| tú | pediste | sentiste | dormiste |
| él/ella/usted | pidió | sintió | durmió |
| nosotros/as | pedimos | sentimos | dormimos |
| vosotros/as | pedisteis | sentisteis | dormisteis |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | pidieron | sintieron | durmieron |

**Table.** *(irregular stem + shared endings; e.g. estar → estuv‑)*

| Person | ending | estar (estuv‑) | tener (tuv‑) | hacer (hic‑/hiz‑) | poder (pud‑) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | ‑e | estuve | tuve | hice | pude |
| tú | ‑iste | estuviste | tuviste | hiciste | pudiste |
| él/ella/usted | ‑o | estuvo | tuvo | hizo | pudo |
| nosotros/as | ‑imos | estuvimos | tuvimos | hicimos | pudimos |
| vosotros/as | ‑isteis | estuvisteis | tuvisteis | hicisteis | pudisteis |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | ‑ieron | estuvieron | tuvieron | hicieron | pudieron |

**Table.** *(more irregular stems, same endings — and ser/ir)*

| Verb | stem | yo form | 3rd sing. | 3rd plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| poner | pus‑ | puse | puso | pusieron |
| querer | quis‑ | quise | quiso | quisieron |
| saber | sup‑ | supe | supo | supieron |
| venir | vin‑ | vine | vino | vinieron |
| decir | dij‑ | dije | dijo | dijeron |
| ser / ir | fu‑ | fui | fue | fueron |

**Ojo.** In the irregular-stem group the stress is **not** on the ending, so *yo* and *él* forms have **no accent**: *estuve, estuvo* (compare regular *comí, comió*). Stems ending in ‑j (*dij‑*) drop the *i* of the ending: *dijeron*, not *dijieron*.

#### G · Indefinido vs imperfecto {#cat=pasado}

**Rule.** When telling a story, both past tenses work together. The **pretérito indefinido** presents completed events that **move the story forward** ("what happened next"). The **pretérito imperfecto** paints the **background** — circumstances, descriptions, weather, ongoing situations, feelings — while the story pauses. A single sentence often has both: the scene (imperfecto) and the event (indefinido).

**Examples.**
- **Hacía** buen tiempo, así que **decidí** salir. — *The weather was nice, so I decided to go out.*
- Cuando **llegué** a casa, mi hermana **dormía**. — *When I arrived home, my sister was sleeping.*
- **Estábamos** cenando cuando **sonó** el teléfono. — *We were having dinner when the phone rang.*
- El hombre **llevaba** una camisa verde y **tenía** un libro; de repente, **empezó** a correr. — *The man was wearing a green shirt and had a book; suddenly, he started to run.*
- **Era** de noche y no **había** nadie en la calle. — *It was night and there was no one in the street.*

**Table.**

| Pretérito indefinido | Pretérito imperfecto |
|---|---|
| completed events that advance the story | background, description, circumstances |
| "what happened" | "what was going on / what things were like" |
| Salí, vi, entró, desapareció | hacía sol, había gente, era tímido |
| single, finished actions | ongoing or repeated situations |
| markers: de repente, entonces, luego | markers: mientras, siempre, todos los días |

**Ojo.** The same verb can appear in either tense depending on your focus: *estuvo lloviendo dos horas* (a finished stretch of rain = event) vs *estaba lloviendo y no salí* (rain as background = circumstance). Think: does it **push the plot** (indefinido) or **set the scene** (imperfecto)?

#### G · The gerundio and estar + gerundio {#cat=imperativo-condicional-gerundio}

**Rule.** The gerundio (‑ando / ‑iendo, English "‑ing") joins **estar** to show an action **in progress**. Put *estar* in the past tense you need: **imperfecto** (*estaba*) for an ongoing background action, **indefinido** (*estuve*) for a completed stretch of time, or **pretérito perfecto** (*he estado*) for something done over a recent period. To say an action was **absent**, use *estar sin* + infinitive.

**Examples.**
- **Estaba** lloviendo y no salí de casa. — *It was raining and I didn't leave the house.* (background)
- Ayer **estuve** hablando con Paco toda la tarde. — *Yesterday I was talking with Paco all afternoon.* (finished stretch)
- Estos días **he estado** pensando en ti. — *These days I've been thinking about you.* (recent period)
- Cuando me llamaste, **estaba** cenando. — *When you called me, I was having dinner.*
- **He estado** todo el fin de semana **sin** salir de casa. — *I've been all weekend without leaving home.*

**Table.** *(forming the gerundio)*

| Verb type | Gerundio ending | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| ‑ar | ‑ando | hablar → hablando | speaking |
| ‑er | ‑iendo | comer → comiendo | eating |
| ‑ir | ‑iendo | vivir → viviendo | living |
| irregular (vowel change) | ‑yendo / u,i | leer → leyendo, dormir → durmiendo | reading, sleeping |

**Table.** *(estar + gerundio across the past tenses)*

| Tense of estar | Example | Use | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| imperfecto (estaba) | estaba estudiando | ongoing / background | I was studying |
| indefinido (estuve) | estuve estudiando cinco años | completed stretch | I studied for five years |
| pretérito perfecto (he estado) | he estado estudiando | recent period | I've been studying |

**Ojo.** Some gerundios are irregular: *leer → leyendo, oír → oyendo, ir → yendo, decir → diciendo, pedir → pidiendo, dormir → durmiendo*. Pronouns can attach to the gerundio (with an accent): *estaba lavándome* = *me estaba lavando*.

#### G · Narrative time markers {#cat=numeros-tiempo}

**Rule.** These connectors organise an anecdote and keep the listener oriented in time. Some open a story, some sequence the events, and some signal a sudden turn. They pair naturally with the indefinido (events) and imperfecto (background).

**Examples.**
- **Un día**, cuando volvía a casa, encontré una cartera en la calle. — *One day, on my way home, I found a wallet in the street.*
- Abrí la puerta y, **entonces**, oí un ruido raro. — *I opened the door and then I heard a strange noise.*
- **Luego** / **Más tarde** llegó Laura y vio la sorpresa. — *Later Laura arrived and saw the surprise.*
- **De repente**, la gente empezó a poner caras extrañas. — *Suddenly, people started pulling strange faces.*
- **Resulta que** era el cumpleaños de mi amiga. — *It turns out it was my friend's birthday.*

**Table.**

| Marker | Function | English |
|---|---|---|
| Una vez / Un día / El otro día | opens the story | Once / One day / The other day |
| (Y) entonces / en ese momento | sequences (then) | (And) then / at that moment |
| Luego / Más tarde / Después | sequences (later) | Then / Later / Afterwards |
| De repente | sudden event | Suddenly |
| Resulta que | reveals an explanation | It turns out that |

**Ojo.** *Resulta que* introduces a surprising fact or the "twist" of the story — it doesn't mean "as a result". *Entonces* can mean both "at that moment" and "so/therefore"; context tells you which.

### En contexto

**Diálogo — Contando una anécdota**
> **Sara:** ¿Sabes qué me pasó el otro día?
> **Nacho:** No, cuéntame.
> **Sara:** Pues estaba paseando por el parque cuando vi a un hombre muy raro.
> **Nacho:** ¿Y qué hizo?
> **Sara:** Nada, pero de repente empezó a correr y desapareció entre los árboles.
> **Nacho:** ¡Qué miedo! ¿Y tú qué hiciste?
> **Sara:** Me asusté mucho y volví a casa corriendo. Resulta que era mi vecino.
> **Nacho:** ¡Qué corte! ¿No lo reconociste?
> **Sara:** ¡No! Estaba muy oscuro y no llevaba las gafas.

*Translation:* Sara: Do you know what happened to me the other day? — Nacho: No, tell me. — Sara: Well, I was walking through the park when I saw a very strange man. — Nacho: And what did he do? — Sara: Nothing, but suddenly he started to run and disappeared among the trees. — Nacho: How scary! And what did you do? — Sara: I got really frightened and ran home. It turns out it was my neighbour. — Nacho: How embarrassing! Didn't you recognise him? — Sara: No! It was very dark and I wasn't wearing my glasses.

**More examples:**
- Aquel día estuve horas esperando, pero el tren nunca llegó. — *That day I waited for hours, but the train never came.*
- Cuando era pequeño, viví un terremoto y pasé mucho miedo. — *When I was little, I lived through an earthquake and I was very scared.*
- Preparé una tarta, pero, sin darme cuenta, le puse sal en vez de azúcar. — *I made a cake, but without realising it I put salt in instead of sugar.*
- Estábamos bailando cuando, de repente, se fue la luz. — *We were dancing when suddenly the lights went out.*
- Hizo mucho calor todo el día y al final tuvimos una tormenta. — *It was very hot all day and in the end we had a storm.*
- Fui a buscar a mi amigo, pero no me abrió la puerta. — *I went to look for my friend, but he didn't open the door.*
- Resulta que se me rompió el vestido y no me di cuenta. — *It turns out my dress ripped and I didn't notice.*

**Truco.** Picture a film: the **imperfecto** is the set, the weather and the mood that stay on screen (*era de noche, llovía, estaba nervioso*), while the **indefinido** is each new action that makes the plot jump forward (*abrí, vi, salí corriendo*).
