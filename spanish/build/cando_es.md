# Can-do promises (one per unit)

## En el aula {#u=A1-0}
- Greet someone and say goodbye at the right time of day, and ask how they are.
- Say you don't understand, and ask someone to repeat, slow down, or say it another way.
- Ask what something is called in Spanish and how a word is spelled, and spell your own name out loud.
- Count from 0 to 10 and understand simple classroom instructions.

## Nosotros y nosotras {#u=A1-1}
- Introduce yourself and someone else: name, age, where you're from, your nationality and what you do.
- Ask another person those same questions and understand their answers.
- Fill in a simple form with your name, nationality, age, phone number and address.
- Count to 100 and give your phone number and your age.
- Reply to simple direct questions about yourself when someone speaks slowly and clearly.

## Quiero aprender español {#u=A1-2}
- Say which languages you speak and how well, and explain why you're learning Spanish.
- Talk about what you do in class and in your free time, using the present tense.
- Say what you like and don't like about learning a language.
- Use *el/la/un/una* correctly and make words agree, so people understand exactly what you mean.

## ¿Dónde está Santiago? {#u=A1-3}
- Say where a country, region or city is and what it's near.
- Describe a place in simple terms: big, mountainous, cold, full of people.
- Say what the weather is like now and what it's usually like in each season.
- Ask and answer *where*, *how*, *how many* and *what's it like* questions about places.
- Understand short, simple descriptions of places, especially with a map or pictures beside them.

## ¿Cuál prefieres? {#u=A1-4}
- Make a simple purchase: say what you want, ask the price, and say which one you prefer.
- Point things out and ask about them: *this one, that one, which one?*
- Describe clothes and objects by colour, size and material, and compare two of them.
- Handle numbers, quantities and prices above 100, and understand a price said to you.
- Say what you have to buy or do, using *tener que*.

## Tus amigos son mis amigos {#u=A1-5}
- Describe your family: who they are, how old they are, what they're like.
- Say what you like, love and don't like, and how much.
- Agree and disagree with someone else's tastes (*a mí también / a mí tampoco / a mí sí*).
- Say whose something is, using *mi, tu, su, nuestro*.
- Describe a person's character in simple terms, softening it with *muy, bastante, un poco*.

## Día a día {#u=A1-6}
- Describe your daily routine from getting up to going to bed.
- Tell the time and say at what time you do things.
- Say how often you do something: always, sometimes, never, twice a week.
- Put your day in order using *primero, después, luego, por último*.
- Ask someone else about their habits and routines, and understand the answer.

## ¡A comer! {#u=A1-7}
- Order a starter, a main course, a drink and a dessert in a bar or restaurant.
- Ask what a dish is made of, and say what's in yours.
- Say how you like things cooked, without repeating the name of the food.
- Read a Spanish menu and find what you want on it.
- Ask for the bill and ask how much it is.

## El barrio ideal {#u=A1-8}
- Describe your neighbourhood: what there is, what there isn't, what there's too much of.
- Ask whether there's a chemist, a bank or a supermarket nearby, and understand the answer.
- Give and follow simple directions: straight on, second on the left, opposite the church.
- Say exactly where something is: next to, near, far from, behind, on the corner.
- Understand everyday street signs and notices in public places.

## ¿Sabes conducir? {#u=A1-9}
- Say what you can do and what you know how to do, and ask someone else.
- Talk about things you have done and places you have been (*he trabajado, he vivido, ¿has estado alguna vez…?*).
- Describe your job or your studies and the qualities they need.
- Answer simple questions about yourself in a job interview, and ask a few back.
- Describe yourself to someone who doesn't know you: what you're good at, what you're like.

## El español y tú {#u=A2-1}
- Say how long you've been learning Spanish and how long you've been doing other things (*desde, desde hace, hace… que*).
- Say how you feel about learning: what's hard, what you enjoy, what makes you nervous.
- Explain why you're learning and what you want to use Spanish for.
- Talk about what you do to learn, and ask someone else what works for them.

## Una vida de película {#u=A2-2}
- Tell the story of someone's life: born, studied, moved, started work, died.
- Say when things happened, using dates, years and past time markers.
- Write a short, simple biography of a real or imaginary person.
- Say how long something lasted and when it started and finished (*desde, durante, hasta*).
- Give short, basic descriptions of past events and activities.

## Hogar, dulce hogar {#u=A2-3}
- Describe where you live: the type of home, the rooms, what's in them.
- Say exactly where furniture and objects are in a room.
- Compare two homes or two objects: bigger, smaller, more expensive, the same as.
- Say what's yours and what's someone else's (*el mío, la tuya, el suyo*).
- Explain what you like, love and prefer about a place, and why.

## ¿Cómo va todo? {#u=A2-4}
- Greet, introduce and say goodbye appropriately, formally and informally.
- Ask for something politely, and ask someone for a favour (*¿Puedes…? ¿Te importa…? ¿Podrías…?*).
- Accept, refuse and apologise, and respond when someone thanks or apologises to you.
- Say what someone is doing right now (*está trabajando, estoy comiendo*).
- Lend, give and borrow things, and ask for them back.

## Guía del ocio {#u=A2-5}
- Suggest what to do this evening or at the weekend, and respond to someone else's suggestion.
- Make arrangements: when, where and what time to meet.
- Say what you're going to do, what you want to do and what you're thinking of doing.
- Say what you've already done and what you haven't done yet (*ya / todavía no*).
- Find what you need in a simple listings guide: times, prices, places.

## No como carne {#u=A2-6}
- Say what you do and don't eat, and explain why.
- Explain how a dish is made, step by step, using the impersonal *se*.
- Ask for a quantity: a kilo, half a litre, two hundred grams, a packet.
- Talk about food without repeating the names of things (*lo pongo, las corto, se añaden*).
- Follow a simple written recipe or set of instructions.

## Nos gustó mucho {#u=A2-7}
- Say where you went, what you did, and whether you liked it.
- Tell the difference between something you've done at some point and something you did on a particular day.
- Give an opinion about a place, a film or a meal, and react with an exclamation (*¡Qué bonito! ¡Qué bien!*).
- Say what you'd like to do (*me gustaría ir a…*).
- Say whether you got on with someone and whether you had a good time.

## Estamos muy bien {#u=A2-8}
- Say how you feel and what hurts, and ask someone else what's wrong.
- Describe common symptoms at a pharmacy or a doctor's, and ask for something for them.
- Give someone simple advice and instructions using the imperative.
- Tell the difference between what someone is like and how they are today (*es nervioso / está nervioso*).
- Wish someone well and react appropriately to bad news.

## Antes y ahora {#u=A2-9}
- Describe what your life used to be like: where you lived, what you did, what you liked.
- Compare then and now (*antes… ahora…, ya no…, todavía…*).
- Describe a person, a place or a situation in the past.
- Talk about the different stages of your life, and ask someone about theirs.
- Write a few linked sentences about your childhood or a past habit.

## Momentos especiales {#u=A2-10}
- Tell a story about something that happened to you, in a simple sequence of points.
- Set the scene and then say what happened (*hacía frío y estábamos cansados, y de repente…*).
- Say what you were doing when something interrupted it.
- Link your story with *primero, entonces, de repente, al final, y, pero, porque*.
- Say how you felt, and react to someone else's story (*¿Sí? ¿De verdad? ¡Qué fuerte!*).
