# Diálogo dirigido — one reads, one talks

The easy way into this book, and the one to start with.

**One of you is the guide.** You hold the page and read the lines out, one at a time. You are never
asked to invent anything — every word you need is printed.

**The other one talks.** No page, no notes, nothing to read. You just answer.

That split is the point. Inventing a whole conversation from nothing is the hardest thing a beginner
can be asked to do; answering a question somebody has just asked you is one of the easiest. The guide
does the hard half, and the guide's half is written down.

## How to run one

1. Decide who guides. The guide takes the book; the talker closes theirs.
2. The guide reads line **1** out loud, and **waits**. Not for a perfect answer — for a whole one.
3. After each line, the small grey text shows roughly what should come back. If nothing comes back,
   use the **Ayuda** phrases at the bottom, then read the example answer out and move on.
4. Work down to the last line. Four or five minutes.
5. **Now swap, and run version B.** Same unit, same grammar, different questions and different
   details — so the person who was listening a moment ago cannot simply repeat what they heard.

## Two rules

**The guide does not correct mid-answer.** Let a wrong sentence finish. Correcting inside a sentence
is the fastest way to make somebody stop talking. If something needs saying, say it at the end.

**The talker answers in whole sentences.** *Barcelona* is not an answer. *Soy de Barcelona* is. The
guide can just ask the line again to get one.

## Unidad 0 — En el aula {#nivel=A1 #unidad=0}

### A · En clase

**Líneas.**
1. Buenos días. ¿Cómo te llamas? → Me llamo ___ .
2. ¿Cómo se escribe? → Pe, a, pe, e, ele…
3. ¿Qué hay en la mochila? → En la mochila hay ___ .
4. ¿Hay un libro? → Sí, hay uno. / No, no hay libros.
5. ¿Hay bolígrafos? → Sí, hay ___ . / No hay.
6. ¿Y en la mesa? → En la mesa hay ___ .
7. ¿Hay una tableta? → Sí, hay una. / No, no hay.
8. Muy bien. ¡Gracias, hasta luego! → ¡Hasta luego!

**Ayuda.** ¿Puedes repetir, por favor? · Más despacio, por favor. · Por ejemplo: «Hay dos cuadernos».
