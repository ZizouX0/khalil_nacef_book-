# The Spanish You Already Know

Here's a secret: **you already know hundreds of Spanish words.** English and Spanish share thousands of near-identical words called **cognates** (*cognados*). Spot the pattern and your vocabulary jumps overnight.

## Words that are almost identical
| Español | English | Español | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| el hospital | hospital | el animal | animal |
| el hotel | hotel | el restaurante | restaurant |
| la familia | family | el doctor | doctor |
| la música | music | el actor | actor |
| el teléfono | telephone | el problema | problem |
| la idea | idea | el chocolate | chocolate |
| natural | natural | posible | possible |
| importante | important | terrible | terrible |
| fantástico | fantastic | diferente | different |

## Endings that convert automatically
| English ending | → Spanish | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **-tion** | **-ción** | information → la informa**ción**, nation → la na**ción** |
| **-ty** | **-dad** | university → la universi**dad**, city → la ciu**dad** |
| **-ly** | **-mente** | normally → normal**mente**, rapidly → rápida**mente** |
| **-ous** | **-oso/-osa** | famous → fam**oso**, delicious → delici**oso** |
| **-ant / -ent** | **-ante / -ente** | important → import**ante**, different → difer**ente** |
| **-ary** | **-ario** | necessary → neces**ario**, ordinary → ordin**ario** |

> **¡Ojo!** They're spelled alike but **pronounced differently** (Spanish vowels are pure, and the stress often moves): *hotel* = "oh-TEL", *animal* = "ah-nee-MAL", *doctor* = "dok-TOR". Read them with Spanish sounds (see *Pronunciation & Accents*).

# False Friends (*los falsos amigos*)

A few words **look** English but mean something completely different. These trip up every learner — memorise them now and avoid embarrassing mix-ups.

| Spanish word | **Really means** | NOT… (that word is…) |
|---|---|---|
| **embarazada** | pregnant | ~~embarrassed~~ → *avergonzado/a* |
| **éxito** | success | ~~exit~~ → *la salida* |
| **ropa** | clothes | ~~rope~~ → *la cuerda* |
| **sensible** | sensitive | ~~sensible~~ → *sensato/a* |
| **actualmente** | currently, nowadays | ~~actually~~ → *en realidad* |
| **librería** | bookshop | ~~library~~ → *la biblioteca* |
| **carpeta** | folder | ~~carpet~~ → *la alfombra* |
| **constipado** | having a cold | ~~constipated~~ → *estreñido* |
| **largo** | long | ~~large~~ → *grande* |
| **asistir (a)** | to attend | ~~assist~~ → *ayudar* |
| **realizar** | to carry out | ~~realize~~ → *darse cuenta* |
| **introducir** | to insert | ~~introduce a person~~ → *presentar* |

> **Classic mistake:** *"Estoy embarazada"* means **"I'm pregnant"**, not "I'm embarrassed"! To say you're embarrassed: *"Estoy avergonzado/a"* or *"Qué vergüenza."*
