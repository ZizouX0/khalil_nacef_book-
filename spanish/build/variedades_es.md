# Which Spanish Are You Learning?

Spanish is one language with about 500 million speakers across 21 countries, and they all understand each
other. But there are real differences, and this book tells you which side of them it is on.

**This book teaches the Spanish of Spain** — because that is the variety of the course it is built on, and
because if you learn it, everyone from Tijuana to Tierra del Fuego will understand you.

## Four things to know from day one

**1. "You all" is not the same everywhere.**
Spain uses **vosotros** informally and **ustedes** formally. **All of Latin America uses only *ustedes*** —
for everyone, always. That is why every verb table in this book has both rows. If you are heading to Latin
America, you may simply ignore the *vosotros* row and you will never be wrong.

**2. Vos.**
In Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, most of Central America and parts of Colombia, informal "you" is **vos**,
not *tú* — with its own verb forms: *vos tenés, vos hablás, vos sos*. The tables show `tú (vos)` so that you
recognise it when you meet it. You do not have to produce it.

**3. The "th" sound.**
In most of Spain, **c** before *e/i* and **z** sound like English *th*: *cinco* = THEEN-ko, *plaza* =
PLA-tha. In all of Latin America and in southern Spain they sound like **s** — this is called *seseo*.
Both are completely correct.

**4. About 5% of everyday words split.**
Each unit shows you the splits that matter in an **En España / En América** box.

| This book (Spain) | Much of Latin America |
|---|---|
| el coche | el carro, el auto |
| el ordenador | la computadora |
| el móvil | el celular |
| la patata | la papa |
| el zumo | el jugo |
| el billete (bus, train) | el boleto, el pasaje |
| conducir | manejar |
| coger (to take/catch) | tomar — *coger is vulgar in much of Latin America; use **tomar*** |

> **¡Ojo!** *Coger el autobús* is completely normal in Spain and badly rude in Mexico, Argentina and much of
> the region. When in doubt anywhere in the Americas, say **tomar**. This is the one word difference worth
> memorising today.

**Nothing you learn here will be "wrong" anywhere.** You will just sound like you learned in Madrid.
