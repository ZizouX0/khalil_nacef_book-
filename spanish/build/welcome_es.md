# Start Here — How to Use This Book

**¡Hola! Welcome.** This book takes you from **zero** to a solid A2 level in Spanish. You don't need to know a single word to begin — everything is explained in English, step by step, and you learn by *seeing the language in action* and then *practising it yourself*.

## Who this book is for
Complete beginners who want to **learn**, not just revise. If you can read this sentence, you can start Lesson 1.

## How the book is organised
**1. Start Here** (this page) and **Pronunciation & Accents** — read these two first.

**2. The 20 Lessons** — the heart of the book, in learning order (A1 first, then A2). Work through them **one at a time, in order**. Every lesson has the same four parts:

- **Vocabulary** — the words of the topic, shown in context, colour-coded by gender, often with a picture or diagram.
- **Grammar** — the rules you need for this topic, explained simply, with a full table and a *pitfall* warning.
- **Conversations** — a real mini-dialogue using the words and grammar, with the English underneath.
- **Practice** — exercises so *you* use the language. **Do them!** This is where learning really happens.

**3. Reference** (at the back) — grammar tables, a glossary of every verb and adjective, useful phrases, an index, and the **Answer Key** for all the exercises.

## The colours and boxes (your signposts)
| You'll see | It means |
|---|---|
| <span class="gen m">el</span> before a word | this noun is **masculine** |
| <span class="gen f">la</span> before a word | this noun is **feminine** |
| a yellow **TRUCO** box | a memory trick / tip |
| a red **¡OJO!** box | watch out — a common mistake |
| a blue **EN CONTEXTO** box | a real conversation to read aloud |

> **Gender matters in Spanish.** Every noun is masculine or feminine, so always learn a word *with its* **el** *or* **la** — that's why we colour-code them.

## How to study each lesson (7 steps)
1. Read the **Vocabulary** out loud — don't worry about memorising everything at once.
2. Study the **Grammar** boxes slowly; look at every example.
3. Read the **Conversations** dialogue **out loud**, then read the English to check you understood.
4. Do the **Practice** exercises with a pen.
5. Check the **Answer Key** at the back — mark what you got wrong.
6. Re-read the parts you missed.
7. Move to the next lesson only when you feel comfortable. **Little and often beats cramming.**

**A suggested pace:** about **2 lessons a week** (20–30 minutes a day) makes this a ~10-week course. After units A1-5, A1-9, A2-5 and A2-10 you'll find a **Repaso** (review) to check your progress.
