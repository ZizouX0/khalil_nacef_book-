# 🗺️ Start here · Your SIELE materials

You have **5 books, 8 audio tracks** and your own exam books. This page tells you what each one is, when to use it and how.
**Lost?** Open Book 1 → *El SIELE* → **Your 7-day plan**. It tells you what to do every day.

## 📚 The 5 books

| Book | What's inside | When | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Guide** (42 p.) | What to **know and remember**. Every test starts with a **one-page summary**, then one card per task. The 7-day plan, the memory tricks and the flashcards. | **Every day**, 15–20 min. **The night before:** only the 4 one-page summaries. | Read the "on one page" first, then the task cards. Cut out the flashcards and fold them. |
| **2 · Practice** (24 p.) | **Exercises with answers**: reading T1–T3, the **Tareas 4–5 rescue kit**, listening exercises 1–5 with transcripts, speaking prompts. | Every day, following the plan. | Timer on, no dictionary. Then check the key and **name the trap** behind each mistake. |
| **3 · Writing workbook** (41 p.) | **6 days × one full writing exam** (Tarea 1 + Tarea 2). The help fades: Days 1–2 full guide, 3–4 half, 5–6 exam mode. B1 model answers at the end. | **Thu → Tue**, one day each, 50 min. | Write on paper or on a computer, count your words, tick the checklist, then compare with the model. |
| **4 · Speaking book** | **T1** 8 series · **T2** 5 photo pairs · **T3** 6 sets · **T4–T5** 4 sets, each with a **"How to answer"** model. | Every day, 20–30 min. | **Record yourself** with your phone → read the model → record again. |
| **5 · Word & verb bank** | **Link words** that raise your marks, a **phrase bank for every writing and speaking task**, grammar helpers (tenses, subjunctive, *gustar*, prepositions) and **40 verbs fully conjugated**, with the irregular forms in red. | **Open it while you write and speak.** 10 min a day of verbs. | Before writing: pick 3 connectors and 2 phrases from the task's bank. Learn the red forms of 5 verbs a day. |

## 🔊 The 8 audio tracks

| Track | What you hear | Use it with | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Listening Tarea 1 · a conversation | Book 2 · Listening, Ejercicio 1 | Press play and **don't pause**. The 30 s reading time and both plays are built in. |
| **02** | Listening Tarea 2 · 5 radio ads | Book 2 · Ejercicio 2 | Same. Answer during the 10 s after each ad. |
| **03** | Listening **Tarea 3 · Set A** (un viaje) | Book 2 · Ejercicio 3 | Your priority. Use ¿Quién? · ¿Pero…? · ¿Qué tal? and check the GUESTS. |
| **04** | Listening **Tarea 3 · Set B** (el primer día) | Book 2 · Ejercicio 4 | Same. Later, replay it **without** the transcript. |
| **05** | Numbers, times, prices | Book 2 · Ejercicio 5 | Write each number. Then check the transcript. |
| **06** | Speaking T1 simulator (2 series) | Book 2 · Speaking | Answer **at the beep**: 15 / 15 / 30 / 30 s. Record yourself. |
| **07** | Speaking T1 · **series 1–8** | Book 4 · Tarea 1 | Same rhythm: question → 10 s to think → beep → answer → beep. |
| **08** | Speaking T4 · the 3 questions of every set | Book 4 · Tareas 4–5 | First read the option's text in the book (1 min), then answer each question for **1 min**. |

The tracks copy the real exam: same pauses, real-exam speed, voices from Spain, Mexico and Argentina.

## ☀️ One day, the simple version

:::flow
1 · Guide | read today's chapter (15–20')
2 · Listening | 1 track from Book 2 (20–30')
3 · Writing | today's day in Book 3 (50')
4 · Speaking | 1 set in Book 4, recorded (20–30')
5 · Flashcards | 10 minutes before bed
:::

:::remember
Blank = 0. Guess = a free chance. **Tick every point. Fill the clock. Never skip a task.**
:::
