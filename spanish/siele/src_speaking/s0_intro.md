# 🗣️ Speaking book · How to use it

:::stats
5 tareas | in 15–20 minutes, recorded
50 | points per tarea (250 in total)
125 | points = B1 · 76 = A2
:::

:::big
Speaking is **recorded**, so nobody interrupts you.
Your job: **cover every point** and **fill the clock.**
:::

## The 5 tareas at a glance

:::flow
T1 · A1–A2 | 4 personal questions · 15 / 15 / 30 / 30 s
T2 · A2 | describe 1 of 2 photos · PASEO
T3 · B1 | 2 voice messages · 3 points each
T4 · B2 | read a short text · answer 3 questions
T5 · C1 | argue for or against · 2 min to prepare
:::

## How to practise with this book

:::steps Every task, every time
1. **Set a timer** to the time in the exam box, and **record yourself** on your phone.
2. **Answer without reading the model.** Make it a real try.
3. **Read the "How to answer" model.** Tick what you covered and copy one phrase you'll reuse.
4. **Record again.** The second try is where you improve.
:::

:::icons Your 3 tricks
📅 | **HOY · HOY · AYER · MAÑANA**: Tarea 1 tenses (present, present, past, future)
📸 | **PASEO**: Tarea 2 photo (Personas · Aspecto y ropa · Sucede y sienten · En qué lugar · Objetos)
🥪 | **SÁNDWICH**: Tarea 3 message (Hola → porque → 1, 2, 3 → adiós)
🔊 | **Audio track 06**: asks you the Tarea 1 questions with the exam timing (Practice book)
:::

:::tip Self-check after each recording
Did I answer **every** point? · Did I use the **right tense**? · Did I add a **reason** (*porque…*)? · Did I talk until the time ran out? · Did I end properly (*un abrazo · gracias · hasta pronto*)?
:::

:::remember
Silence scores zero. A simple sentence scores. **Keep talking:** *pues… a ver… la verdad es que…*
:::
