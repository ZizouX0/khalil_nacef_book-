# 📖 Comprensión de lectura · Reading

## 📖 Reading on one page

:::stats
60 min | 5 tasks, 38 questions, one clock
5 + 5 + 8 | = 18 = the B1 line (10 = A2)
42 | the minute you leave Tarea 3, no matter what
0 | points lost for a wrong answer
:::

:::golden The 4 rules
1. **Same words = suspicious.** The right option says it in other words: "al lado del metro" → *bien comunicada*.
2. **Siguiente = Salida.** Leave a task and it is gone. Only T1 and T4 have ◀️ *Anterior*.
3. **10 / 12 / 20 min for T1–T3.** Then a guess run: every gap of T4–T5 filled.
4. **Mouse only, no blanks.** The keyboard can change your answers. A guess costs nothing.
:::

Every wrong option is one of the **6 sospechosos**.
Each one takes a real piece of the text and twists it.

:::mnemonic 6 SOSPECHOSOS
🦜 | El Loro | repeats the text's words, but in the wrong place
🎭 | El Impostor | true… but about another person
⏳ | El Viajero | true… but at another time: the past, or only a plan
🔍 | El Zoom | too big or too small: algunos → todos, puede → tiene que
🙃 | Don Contrario | the text says the opposite
👯 | El Gemelo | (T3) another person says something similar
:::

Where they live: **T1** 🦜 🔍 🙃 · **T2** 🎭 ⏳ · **T3** 👯 · and 🦜 everywhere.

:::remember
T1–T3 are your exam. T4–T5 are your bonus.
T1 and T2: the questions follow the text. T3: the questions jump.
:::

## Tarea 1 · A1 · Cinco textos breves

**You get:** 5 short texts (40–80 words), one per screen: notices, ads, messages. One question each, 3 options.

:::flow T1 · minute 0 → 10
screens 1–5 | about 1 min 45 each
minute 9 | ◀️ Anterior: back to your doubts
minute 10 | Siguiente on screen 5 = goodbye T1
:::

:::steps One text, one routine
1. **Name** the text type: title, greeting, signature, prices.
2. **Sort** the question: general (*¿para qué? ¿para quién?*) or detail (time, price, place)?
3. **Test** each option: does the text say this *about this thing*?
4. **Kill** two. Unsure? Prove the other two false (Manual p.22).
:::

:::trap 🦜 El Loro · the parrot
He repeats a real word, but it answers another question.
"el lunes" is when tickets go on sale, not the concert day (Guía p.155).
**Ask:** does this word answer *this* question?
:::

:::trap 🔍 El Zoom · too big, too small
"gusta a pequeños y a grandes" ≠ "especialmente para niños" (Guía p.155).
**Ask:** how many? who exactly? must or may?
:::

:::remember
T1 = 2 general questions + 3 details.
Your only way back: ◀️ Anterior, before screen 5.
:::

## Tarea 2 · A2 · Un correo largo

**You get:** 1 email (250–300 words) on one screen. 5 questions, 3 options. The instruction says who writes to whom.

:::flow T2 · minute 10 → 22
1 min | instruction: who writes to whom?
3 min | skim: ¿quién? ¿a quién? ¿para qué?
1 min | the 5 questions: underline person + time
6 min | answer in text order
1 min | 5 answers? → Siguiente (minute 22)
:::

:::steps The ID-and-clock check
1. **Hunt** the purpose: *Te escribo para…*, *¿Te apetece venir?*
2. **Follow** the text order for the details.
3. **Check** every option: 🪪 this person? 🕐 past, now or plan?
4. **Kill** two (Manual p.26).
:::

:::trap The purpose hides in the middle
Paragraph 1 is small talk: *Perdona…*, news, gossip.
In Modelo 0 the purpose is in paragraph 3 (Guía p.157).
**Fix:** keep reading. Don't answer "para qué" from paragraph 1.
:::

:::trap 🎭 El Impostor · ⏳ El Viajero
In Modelo 0, all 10 wrong options were another person, time or place.
The new job is Eugenia's, not Isabel's. Pedro only *wants* to go, so "tiene entradas" is wrong (Guía p.157).
:::

:::remember
Every option, two checks:
🪪 who? 🕐 when?
:::

## Tarea 3 · B1 · Tres personas, ocho preguntas

**You get:** 3 people, one topic (100–120 words each). 8 questions *"¿Quién…?"*, each with a dropdown. The answer is in **one** text (Guía p.28).

:::flow T3 · minute 22 → 42
1 min | the topic in the instruction
3 min | all 8 questions first: underline the key idea
8 min | the 3 texts: answer the easy ones
6 min | traffic light 🚦 for the rest
2 min | 8 dropdowns full? → Siguiente (minute 42)
:::

:::steps The traffic light 🚦
1. **Take** one hard question.
2. **Hold** it against all 3 texts.
3. **Colour** each text: 🟢 gives the info · 🟡 says something similar · 🔴 doesn't mention it (Manual p.30).
4. **Choose** the 🟢. The 🟡 is El Gemelo. Stuck? Skip: the key may be in another text.
:::

:::trap 👯 El Gemelo · the look-alike
Sara: "se lo aconsejo a todo el mundo" → *she* advises.
Gabriela's friends advised *her*. So "¿…porque se lo aconsejaron?" = Gabriela (Guía p.158).
**Ask:** who does what to whom?
:::

:::trap Don't share out the sweets
The answers are not balanced. Modelo 0: Gabriela 4, Paula 2, Sara 2 (Guía p.183).
**Fix:** never share them out to make it fair.
:::

:::remember
T3 = hunt by meaning, not by order.
Minute 42: Siguiente. At 0:00 the test ends, and T4–T5 never appear.
:::

## Tareas 4–5 · B2–C1 · Survival

Above your level? Try anyway: the next task may suit you better.
Your goal: **14 minutes, 20 answers, 0 blanks.**

:::tip Stuck on T4–T5? Use the Rescue kit
Book 2 · Practice has a **Tareas 4 y 5 Rescue kit**. It gives the **GANCHO** method for T4 (G género · A apuntadores · N nexos · C cronología · H hilo · O oración partida), the 3 families of T5 (4 grammar, 4 vocabulary, 4 connectors), and 3 practice texts with every answer explained.
:::

:::steps How to grab easy points
1. **T4 · minute 42 → 49.** 2 texts, 4 gaps each, 5 fragments (1 extra). Hunt pointing words: *esto, esta idea, en ellos, por eso*. Use 4 **different** fragments.
2. **Spot** 🚪 El Colado, the extra fragment. In Modelo 0 it started with a connector that didn't fit: *Por consiguiente…*, *No obstante…*
3. **T5 · minute 49 → 56.** 12 gaps, 3 words each. Read just before and after the gap: a preposition (*servicios **para** más de cien millones*) or *que* + subjunctive (*sería necesario que se **generara***).
4. **Fill** every gap, then Siguiente. A blank is 0. A guess is maybe 1 (Guía p.159–161).
:::

## 💬 Words that decide the answer

:::phrases Read these small words twice
Se alquila · Se vende · Se busca | For rent · For sale · Wanted | T1 purpose
Entrada libre / gratuita | Free entry | free for who?
para mayores de 16 · menores de 12 | over 16 · under 12 | age trap
Venta de entradas en… | Tickets sold at… | ≠ where it happens
Es obligatorio · No se permite | Required · Not allowed | must or may?
22:00 → por la noche | numbers become words | T1
pero · sin embargo · aunque | but · however · although | what follows decides
pensaba / iba a… pero | I was going to… but | the plan changed
ya no · todavía | no longer · still | now or before?
solo · excepto · nunca · nadie | only · except · never · nobody | size
había + participio | had done | B1
lo pasó muy bien → estuvo muy contento | had a great time | T2
quería escapar del día a día → buscaba un cambio en su vida | wanted a change | T3
:::
