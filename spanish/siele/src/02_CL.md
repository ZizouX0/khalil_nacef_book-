:::stats
60 min | one clock for all 5 tasks
38 | questions, all multiple choice
18 | correct answers = B1
0 | points lost for a wrong answer
:::

:::golden The 4 golden rules
1. **Same meaning beats same words.** The right option says the idea with other words (Guía p.42).
2. **Siguiente = Salida.** Leave a task and it is gone for good (Guía p.37).
3. **No blanks.** A wrong answer costs 0 points (Guía p.36).
4. **Mouse only.** The keyboard can change answers you already chose (Manual p.19).
:::

Same words = suspicious.
Same meaning = correct.
:::

## 🗺️ The map

| Tarea | Level | You read | Items |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | A2 | 1 email (250–300 words) | 5 · 3 options |
| 3 | B1 | 3 people, one topic (100–120 words each) | 8 · "¿Quién…?" |
| 4 | B2 | 2 texts, 4 gaps each | 8 · 5 fragments, 1 extra |
| 5 | C1 | 1 specialised text, 12 gaps | 12 · 3 words each |

(Guía p.17; Manual p.19)

:::big
5 + 5 + 8 = 18
T1–T3 = 18 items = the B1 line.
:::

B1 starts at 18 correct. So T1–T3 are your exam. T4–T5 are your bonus.

:::scale Correct answers (of 38) → level
below A1 | 0–4
A1 | 5–9
A2 | 10–17 | safe
B1 | 18–26 | target
B2 | 27–32
C1 | 33–38
:::

In points (of 250): A2 = 66–117, B1 = 118–177 (Guía p.36).

:::tip The free 5 points
Blind guesses on all of T4–T5 give about **5 points** on average (our maths, not official).
So **13 of 18 in T1–T3 + guesses ≈ B1.** But luck is luck: make T1–T3 solid.
:::

## 🕵️ Meet the 6 suspects

Every wrong option is one of six characters. Each one takes a real piece of the text and twists it (Modelo 0, Guía p.155–158).

:::mnemonic 6 SOSPECHOSOS
🦜 | El Loro | repeats the text's words, but in the wrong place
🎭 | El Impostor | true… but about another person
⏳ | El Viajero | true… but at another time: the past, or only a plan
🔍 | El Zoom | too big or too small: algunos → todos, puede → tiene que
🙃 | Don Contrario | the text says the opposite
👯 | El Gemelo | (T3) another person says something similar
:::

Where they live: **T1** 🦜 🔍 🙃 · **T2** 🎭 ⏳ · **T3** 👯 · and 🦜 everywhere.

The right option is the quiet one in disguise: **new words, same meaning.**

:::phrases Text → right option (Modelo 0)
al lado del metro y de paradas de autobús → bien comunicada | good transport links | T1
lo pasó muy bien → estuvo muy contento | had a great time | T2
quería escapar del día a día → buscaba un cambio en su vida | wanted a change | T3
:::

## Tarea 1 · A1 · Cinco textos breves

5 short texts, one per screen: messages, ads, posters, notices.
**2 + 3:** 2 general questions (*¿para qué? ¿para quién?*), 3 details (time, price, place).
The answer is always in the text (Guía p.21).

:::flow T1 in 10 minutes
screens 1–5 | about 1 min 45 each
minute 9 | ◀️ Anterior: back to your doubts
minute 10 | Siguiente on screen 5 = goodbye T1
:::

:::steps One text, one routine
1. **Name** the text type: title, greeting, signature, prices.
2. **Read** it once for the gist.
3. **Sort** the question: general or detail?
4. **Test** each option: does the text say this *about this thing*?
5. **Kill** two. Unsure? Prove the other two false (Manual p.22).
:::

:::trap 🦜 El Loro · the parrot
He repeats a real word, but it answers another question.
Modelo 0: "el lunes" is when tickets go on sale, not the concert day (Guía p.155).
**Ask:** does this word answer *this* question?
:::

:::trap 🔍 El Zoom · too big, too small
He changes the size: *algunos → todos*, *puede → tiene que*.
"gusta a pequeños y a grandes" ≠ "especialmente para niños" (Guía p.155).
**Ask:** how many? who exactly? must or may?
:::

:::trap 🙃 Don Contrario · the opposite
"Concierto para mayores de 16 años" ≠ "los niños pueden entrar" (Guía p.156).
**Ask:** yes or no?
:::

:::tip Numbers become words
"22:00" → "por la noche". Two timetable lines → "horario diferente los fines de semana".
:::

:::remember
T1 = 2 general + 3 details.
Your only way back: ◀️ Anterior, before screen 5.
:::

📚 **Practise:** Manual p.21–23 (Act. 2: underline the proof; *Manos a la obra*); Guía p.155–156.

## Tarea 2 · A2 · Un correo largo

1 email (250–300 words), 5 questions, one screen. The instruction says who writes to whom (Guía p.24).
The email's shape: **a story → likes → a plan** (Guía p.39).

:::flow T2 in 12 minutes
1 min | instruction: who → whom?
3 min | skim: ¿quién? ¿a quién? ¿para qué?
1 min | the 5 questions: underline person + time
6 min | answer in text order
1 min | 5 answers? → Siguiente (minute 22)
:::

:::steps The ID-and-clock check
1. **Find** the purpose. It is often NOT in paragraph 1.
2. **Follow** the text order for the details.
3. **Check** every option: the ID 🪪 (this person?) and the clock 🕐 (past, now or plan?).
4. **Kill** two (Manual p.26).
:::

:::trap 🎭 El Impostor · wrong person
True… but about someone else.
Modelo 0: the new job is Eugenia's, not Isabel's (Guía p.157).
**Ask:** ¿QUIÉN?
:::

:::trap ⏳ El Viajero · wrong time
Already over, or only a plan.
"Estaba muy ocupada con los exámenes": the exams are over (Guía p.157). Pedro only *wants* to go, so "tiene entradas" is wrong.
**Ask:** ¿CUÁNDO?
:::

:::tip The purpose hides in the middle
Paragraph 1 is small talk: *Perdona…*, news, gossip.
The purpose comes later: *Te escribo para…*, *¿Te apetece venir?* In Modelo 0 it is in paragraph 3 (Guía p.157).
:::

Modelo 0 T2: all 10 wrong options are another person, time or place.

:::remember
Every option, two checks:
🪪 who? 🕐 when?
:::

📚 **Practise:** Manual p.25–27 (**Act. 3: say why each wrong option is false**, the best trap trainer); Guía p.157.

## Tarea 3 · B1 · Tres personas, ocho preguntas

3 people, one topic (100–120 words each). 8 questions *"¿Quién…?"*, each with a dropdown.
The answer is in **one** text. The other two usually mention something related (Guía p.28).

:::big
T1 and T2: the questions follow the text.
T3: the questions jump.
:::

:::flow T3 in 20 minutes
1 min | the topic in the instruction
3 min | all 8 questions first: underline the key idea
8 min | the 3 texts: answer the easy ones
6 min | traffic light for the rest
2 min | 8 dropdowns full? → Siguiente (minute 42)
:::

:::steps The traffic light 🚦
1. **Take** one hard question.
2. **Hold** it against all 3 texts.
3. **Colour** each text: 🟢 gives the info · 🟡 says something similar · 🔴 doesn't mention it (Manual p.30).
4. **Choose** the 🟢. The 🟡 is the Gemelo.
5. **Skip** if stuck. The key may be in another text.
:::

:::trap 👯 El Gemelo · the look-alike
Another person uses the same word, with a different meaning.
Sara: "se lo aconsejo a todo el mundo" → *she* advises. Gabriela's friends advised *her*. So "¿…porque se lo aconsejaron?" = Gabriela (Guía p.158).
**Ask:** who does what to whom? *aconsejo* → · *me aconsejaron* ←
:::

:::trap Don't share out the sweets
The answers are not balanced. Modelo 0: Gabriela 4, Paula 2, Sara 2 (solutions p.183).
Never share them out to make it fair.
:::

:::remember
T3 = hunt by meaning, not by order.
One text is 🟢. The others are 🟡 or 🔴.
:::

📚 **Practise:** Manual p.29–31 (**Act. 3, the three-column table: do it in full**); Guía p.158.

## Tareas 4–5 · B2–C1 · Supervivencia

Above your level? Try anyway: the next task may suit you better (Guía p.38).
Texts can come from any Spanish-speaking country: *ordenador = computadora*, *coche = carro* (Guía p.18).
Your goal: **14 minutes, 20 answers, 0 blanks.**

:::flow Survival in 14 minutes
42–49 min | T4: obvious fragments first, then fill the rest
49–56 min | T5: grammar clues first, guess the rest
56 min | every gap full? → Siguiente
:::

### Tarea 4 · Two texts, four gaps each

5 fragments per text, 1 is extra. Use 4 **different** ones. To move a used fragment, deselect it first (Guía p.29).

:::steps Grab the T4 points
1. **Hunt** pointing words: *esto, esta idea, en ellos, por eso* (Manual p.33).
2. **Match** them to the sentence before the gap. Obvious ones first.
3. **Fill** all 4 gaps. Then go to text 2.
:::

:::trap 🚪 El Colado · the gatecrasher
The extra fragment. In Modelo 0, in both texts, it started with a connector that didn't fit: *Por consiguiente…*, *No obstante…* (Guía p.159–160).
**Ask:** does this connector match the idea before it?
:::

### Tarea 5 · Twelve word gaps

12 gaps × 3 options: always 4 grammar, 4 vocabulary, 4 connectors (Guía p.40). Read just before and after each gap.

:::tip Two mechanical clues
**Preposition:** "servicios ambientales **para** más de cien millones de personas".
**Subjunctive after *que*:** "sería necesario que se **generara**" (Guía p.161).
:::

:::remember
A blank is 0. A guess is maybe 1.
:::

## 💬 Phrases that score

:::phrases Notices and ads (T1)
Se alquila · Se vende · Se busca | For rent · For sale · Wanted | the purpose
Entrada libre / gratuita | Free entry | free for who?
para mayores de 16 · menores de 12 | over 16 · under 12 | age trap
Venta de entradas en… | Tickets sold at… | ≠ where it happens
Es obligatorio · No se permite | Required · Not allowed | must or may?
:::

:::phrases Small words that flip the answer
pero · sin embargo · aunque | but · however · although | what follows decides
pensaba / iba a… pero | I was going to… but | the plan changed
ya no · todavía | no longer · still | now or before?
solo · excepto · nunca · nadie | only · except · never · nobody | size
había + participio | had done | B1
:::

**A2**
- one writer, one reader
- *fui, he ido, voy a ir*
- questions follow the text
---
**B1**
- ==three writers==
- ==*había ido*, *se lo aconsejaron*==
- ==questions jump==
- ==every answer is a paraphrase==
:::
