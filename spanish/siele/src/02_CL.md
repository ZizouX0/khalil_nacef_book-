# 📖 Comprensión de lectura (Reading)

## In 30 seconds

- **60 minutes, 5 tasks, 38 items, all multiple choice.** Every item is worth 1 point and **wrong answers cost nothing**, so never leave anything blank (Guía p.17, p.36; Manual p.19).
- **Your levels are in Tareas 1–3.** A2 = **10–17** correct, B1 = **18–26** correct (Guía p.36; Manual p.41). T1 + T2 + T3 have exactly **18 items** (5 + 5 + 8).
- **You can't go back to an earlier task.** Only T1 (5 screens) and T4 (2 screens) let you move between screens with *Anterior* / *Siguiente*. If you press *Siguiente* on the last screen, you leave the task for good (Guía p.37; Manual p.19–20).
- **The right option paraphrases the text. Wrong options often repeat the text's own words** (Guía p.42; Manual p.22, p.26). Same words = suspicious. Same meaning = correct.
- **In T1 and T2 the questions follow the order of the text. In T3 they don't** (Guía p.42; Manual p.28).
- **Your time plan:** about 10 min for T1, 12 for T2 and 20 for T3, then keep **at least 12–15 min** to put an answer in every gap of T4 and T5.
- **Use the mouse only.** The keyboard can change answers you've already chosen (Manual p.19, p.40).

## How the test works

| Tarea | Nivel | What you get | Items | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A1 | 5 short texts of 40–80 words each (messages, emails, ads, notices, activity programmes, blogs/forums), one per screen | 5 (1 per text, 3 options) | Choose the right ending of a sentence. 2 items ask about the general idea, 3 about details |
| 2 | A2 | 1 letter or email, 250–300 words | 5 (3 options) | 2 general items (purpose, people) and 3 detail items |
| 3 | B1 | 3 texts of 100–120 words each, by 3 people on the same topic | 8 | Match each "¿Quién…?" question to one person using a dropdown (*--Elija--*) |
| 4 | B2 | 2 texts of 230–280 words each, each with 4 gaps | 8 | For each text, put 4 of the 5 fragments into the gaps (1 fragment is extra) |
| 5 | C1 (2 items B2, 10 items C1) | 1 specialised text, 375–425 words, with 12 gaps | 12 | Choose 1 of 3 words for each gap (4 grammar, 4 vocabulary, 4 connectors) |

Sources: Guía p.17, 19, 23, 26, 29, 33, 35; Manual p.19–20, 24, 28, 32, 36.

> **Remember:** The Manual's summary table (p.19) says T4 is "1 texto de 230–280 palabras". Its own T4 pages (p.32) and the Guía (p.29) show **two** texts of 230–280 words each, with 4 gaps each. Two texts is correct.

**Scoring** (Guía p.36; Manual p.41; Edelsa, *Sugerencias y consejos*):

| Correct answers (of 38) | Score (of 250) | Level |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | 0–32.99 | below A1 |
| 5–9 | 33–65.99 | A1 |
| **10–17** | **66–117.99** | **A2** |
| **18–26** | **118–177.99** | **B1** |
| 27–32 | 178–216.99 | B2 |
| 33–38 | 217–250 | C1 |

**Interface and timing**
- No task has its own time limit. You share 60 minutes across all five tasks. The timer is always visible at the top right (Guía p.19; Manual p.19).
- Reading the general instruction screen before the test does **not** use up test time (Guía p.41).
- On one-screen tasks (T2, T3, T5) you can change any answer until you press *Siguiente*. If you leave an item blank, a warning appears: **Sí** = move on anyway, **No** = go back and answer (Guía p.38).
- Within a task you can answer in any order. Do the easiest items first (Manual p.19).
- The test only ends when you have gone through every task or the time runs out (Guía p.38). **If the time runs out while you're in T3, you lose your free guesses in T4 and T5.**
- The Guía's default split (for someone doing everything) is **10 / 10 / 12 / 14 / 14 min** (Guía p.41). The Manual says to plan by your target level (Manual p.19). For an A2–B1 target, I suggest:

| T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 | T5 | Buffer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 min | 12 min | 20 min | 7 min | 7 min | 4 min |

> **Tip:** Set two checkpoints. At **22 minutes used** you should be leaving T2. At **42 minutes used**, leave T3 no matter what, so you have time to fill every gap in T4 and T5.

> **Remember:** A blind guess on every T4/T5 item gives about **5 points on average** (my own maths, not an official figure: 12 × ⅓ ≈ 4 in T5, and about 1–2 in T4). So roughly **13 of 18 in T1–T3 plus your T4/T5 guesses** puts you near B1. Guessing is luck, though. Make T1–T3 solid first.

## Task by task

### The trap catalogue (T1–T3)

In T1 and T2 the wrong options "refer to something the text says, but are clearly false" (Guía p.21, p.25). In T3 the other two texts "mention something related" (Guía p.28). From the Modelo 0 answers (Guía p.155–158; solutions p.183), the traps fall into six types. *Spoiler: do Guía p.155–158 yourself first if you want it fresh.*

| Trap | How it works | Modelo 0 example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Echo word** | The option reuses a word from the text, but that word answers a different question | "el lunes" is the day the tickets go on sale, not the day of the concert (T1-1). "centro comercial" is where you buy tickets, not where the concert is (T1-4) |
| **2. Wrong person** | Something true, but about someone else | The new job belongs to Eugenia, not Isabel. The *musicians* arrived late, not Sara's brother (T2-1, T2-3) |
| **3. Wrong time** | It happened in the past, or is only planned or expected | The exams are over ("Estaba muy ocupada…"). The grandparents belong to past summers (T2-4) |
| **4. Too general / too narrow** | "some" becomes "all", or "for everyone" becomes "especially for…" | "gusta a pequeños y a grandes" (children and adults) ≠ "especialmente para niños". Children free with 2 adult tickets ≠ "no cuesta dinero a las familias" (T1-3) |
| **5. Plain contradiction** | The text says the opposite | "mayores de 16 años" ≠ "los niños pueden entrar" (T1-4) |
| **6. Says something similar** (T3) | Another person uses the same word or idea, but not with the meaning the question asks for | Sara writes "se lo aconsejo a todo el mundo" (*she* gives advice). The answer to "¿…porque se lo aconsejaron?" is Gabriela, whose friends told her to go (T3-1) |

> **Trap:** In Modelo 0, the correct option almost never copies the text. "al lado del metro y de algunas paradas de autobuses" became **"una casa bien comunicada"**. "lo pasó muy bien" became **"estuvo muy contento"** (Guía p.155, p.157; solutions p.183).

### Tarea 1 · A1 · Five short texts

- **Format:** 5 texts of 40–80 words on 5 screens, one 3-option question each. The instruction never changes: *"Usted va a leer cinco textos breves. Elija la opción correcta para cada una de las cinco preguntas."* (Guía p.20; Manual p.20).
- **What they test:** 2 items on the general idea (who it's for, why it was written) and 3 on concrete details: times, dates, prices, places (Guía p.21; Manual p.20). There is no inference: the answer is explicit in the text (Guía p.21).
- **Patterns that repeat:**
  - **Text types:** messages and emails between friends, *Se alquila / Se vende* ads, event posters (Lugar, Fecha, Hora, Precio), activity programmes, short notices (Manual p.20; Guía p.20).
  - **Language:** present tense, *haber*, *gustar*, and only the simplest connectors, such as *y* (Guía p.39).
  - **Topics:** numbers, timetables, days and months, rooms of a house, places in town, transport, leisure, shops, food and restaurants, prices, jobs, family and friends (Guía p.39).
  - **Question stems:** *"X dice que…"*, *"X escribe a Y para…"*, *"El anuncio es para personas que quieren…"*, *"En el anuncio se dice que…"*.
  - **Answers turn numbers into words:** "22:00" becomes "por la noche". Two timetable lines become "horario diferente los fines de semana" (Modelo 0, T1-3 and T1-4).
- **Step-by-step strategy** (Manual p.22–23):
  1. Look at the visual clues (title, greeting, signature, prices) and name the text type.
  2. Read the text once for the gist.
  3. Read the stem. Is it a general question (*para qué, para quién*) or a detail (*cuándo, dónde, cuánto*)?
  4. For each option, find the words it echoes, then ask: *does the text say this about this thing?*
  5. If you're unsure, prove the other two false. Aim for about 2 minutes per text.
  6. Before leaving screen 5, use *Anterior* to recheck any doubts. This is the only time you can.
- **Traps:** echo words, especially days, places and prices (types 1 and 5). Also "wants to" vs. "has": in the Guía's version, Pedro only *wants* to go, so "tiene entradas" is wrong (Guía p.22).
- **Practise in the books:** Manual p.21 Act. 2 (underline the words that prove each answer), p.22 Act. 3 (vocabulary by topic), p.23 *Manos a la obra*; Guía p.155–156.

### Tarea 2 · A2 · One long email

- **Format:** 1 letter or email of 250–300 words and 5 questions with 3 options, all on one screen. The instruction changes only in who writes to whom: *"Usted va a leer un correo que Isabel ha escrito a su amiga Sara…"* (Guía p.23–24; Manual p.24).
- **What they test:** 2 items on the main idea (purpose, the relationship between sender and reader) and 3 on simple, explicit details (Guía p.25, p.39).
- **Patterns that repeat:**
  - The email always tells about an **experience**, gives **likes or preferences**, and **proposes a plan** (Guía p.39).
  - **Tenses:** *indefinido, imperfecto, perfecto*, *ir a / querer / poder* + infinitive (Guía p.39). Watch the tense of every option: it is the "wrong time" trap.
  - **General questions** usually come first, or first and last (Guía p.39; Manual p.129).
  - **The purpose is often not in paragraph 1.** In Modelo 0, the email opens with an apology and gossip. The real purpose (*"¿Te apetece venir?"*, an invitation) is in paragraph 3 (Guía p.157).
  - **Topics:** family, trips, friends, short-term plans, work, shopping and free time, celebrations, meals, appointments. Also formal emails about bookings, timetables and payment (Guía p.24, p.39). The Manual's practice email is from a language academy director (p.27).
- **Step-by-step strategy** (Manual p.26):
  1. Underline who writes to whom in the instruction.
  2. Skim the email asking: *¿quién lo escribe? ¿a quién? ¿para qué?*
  3. Read the 5 questions and underline the key words (the person plus the time: *ayer, la próxima semana*).
  4. Detail questions follow the order of the text. One paragraph can answer two questions. The answer to a general question can spread over several paragraphs.
  5. For each option, check **who** and **when**, then eliminate two.
- **Traps:** wrong person and wrong time (types 2 and 3) dominate. In Modelo 0 T2, **every one** of the 10 wrong options is about another person (Eugenia, the musicians), another time (yesterday's lunch, the exams, past summers) or another place (the office, the library) (Guía p.157; solutions p.183). The Manual warns: *"Las opciones incorrectas pueden contener palabras o expresiones empleadas en el texto, pero la información que dan es falsa"* (Manual p.26).
- **Practise in the books:** Manual p.25 Act. 1 (match email openings to their purpose), p.26 Act. 2 (guess the question order) and **Act. 3 (say why each wrong option is false; the best trap trainer)**, p.27 *Manos a la obra*; Guía p.157.

### Tarea 3 · B1 · Three people, eight questions

- **Format:** 3 texts of 100–120 words by three people on one topic (study abroad, blogs, living abroad, trips, hobbies, pets, transport…) and 8 "¿Quién…?" questions, answered with dropdowns on one screen (Guía p.26–27, p.40; Manual p.28).
- **What they test:** finding precise information. The answer is in **only one** text, but the other two usually mention something related (Guía p.28).
- **Patterns that repeat:**
  - **The question order does NOT follow the texts** (Manual p.28).
  - **The answers are not balanced.** In Modelo 0, Gabriela got 4 answers, Paula 2 and Sara 2 (solutions p.183). Never "share out" the answers evenly.
  - **The answer is always a paraphrase:** "escapar del día a día" = "buscaba un cambio en su vida". "aprendí a solucionar los problemas por mí misma" = "ser más independiente". "Pensaba ir… por seis meses, pero… me quedé todo el año" = "cambió de opinión" (Guía p.158).
  - **B1 grammar does the work:** past tenses including the pluperfect (*había hecho*), pronouns (*se lo aconsejaron*: someone advised *her*) and relative clauses (Guía p.40).
  - Typical questions: why they went or started, problems (money, language), what they learned, whether they'd repeat it, a change of plan, who advised whom.
- **Step-by-step strategy** (Manual p.30):
  1. Underline the topic in the instruction.
  2. Read **all 8 questions first** and underline the key idea of each one.
  3. Read the three texts and answer the obvious questions straight away.
  4. For the rest, check all three texts and sort them like the Manual's table: **gives that information / says something similar / doesn't mention it** (Manual p.30).
  5. If you're stuck, skip the question. "La clave para la elección puede estar en otro texto" (Manual p.30).
  6. Check that all 8 dropdowns are filled before *Siguiente*.
- **Traps:** type 6, everywhere. In Modelo 0, two girls talk about money: Paula had money **problems**, while Gabriela **learned to manage** money. Those are two different questions (Guía p.158). Watch who does what to whom (*aconsejo* vs. *me aconsejaron*) and opposites (*me gustaría volver* vs. *no volvería*).
- **Practise in the books:** Manual p.29 Act. 2 (match the questions to anonymous texts), **p.30 Act. 3 (the three-column table; do it in full)**, p.31 *Manos a la obra*; Guía p.158.

### Tarea 4 · B2 · Missing fragments (survival)

- **What it is:** 2 texts (opinion articles, news, travel guides), each with 4 gaps and 5 fragments, one of them extra. You choose fragments in dropdowns. A fragment you've used is crossed out, so to move it you must first deselect it. You can move between the two texts with *Anterior* / *Siguiente* (Guía p.29; Manual p.32).
- **Easy points:** the clues are pronouns, possessives, demonstratives and connectors (Guía p.40; Manual p.33). Look for words that point back to the previous sentence: *esto, esta idea, en ellos, según ésta, por eso*. In Modelo 0, the extra fragment in **both** texts started with a connector whose logic didn't fit (*Por consiguiente…*, *No obstante…*) (Guía p.159–160; solutions p.183).
- **Strategy:** place the most obvious fragment first. Then you have fewer options left for the harder gaps (Manual p.33). **Fill all 4 gaps with 4 different fragments.** About 7 minutes.

### Tarea 5 · C1 · Twelve word gaps (survival)

- **What it is:** a specialised text (science, economy, society) with 12 gaps and 3 options each. There are always 4 grammar items, 4 vocabulary items and 4 connector items (Guía p.33, p.35, p.40). Only 2 items are B2 (Guía p.40).
- **Easy points:** read the words just before and after the gap (Manual p.37). Mechanical grammar clues: a **preposition** (Modelo 0: "servicios ambientales **para** más de cien millones de personas") and **subjunctive after *que*** (Modelo 0: "sería necesario que se **generara**") (Guía p.161; solutions p.183). Then guess the rest. About 7 minutes, and **no blanks**.

## Top tips & tricks

1. **Learn the instructions now.** They are fixed in T1, T4 and T5. In T2 and T3 only the people and topic change, and those tell you who writes and what about (Guía p.37; Manual p.24, p.30).
2. **Answer every single item.** Errors don't subtract (Guía p.36; Manual p.19, p.129; Edelsa, *Sugerencias*).
3. **Never press *Siguiente* until every answer in the task is done and checked.** There is no way back (Guía p.37, p.42; Manual p.19–20).
4. **Mouse only.** Keyboard or arrow keys can change your choices, especially in dropdowns (Manual p.19, p.40).
5. **Plan your 60 minutes by your target level**, and keep time for the T4/T5 guesses (Manual p.19; Guía p.41).
6. **Name the text type first** from its visual clues (title, greeting, signature). Remember that "the information is always in the text", so don't use your general knowledge (Manual p.19–21, p.23).
7. **Same meaning, different words.** "No basta con identificar en el texto una mención…" The correct option "lo expresará con distintas palabras" (Guía p.42; Manual p.22).
8. **In doubt? Prove the other two options false.** And check **who** and **when** in every option (Manual p.22, p.26; Modelo 0, Guía p.157).
9. **Use the text order in T1 and T2** (items follow it, often one per paragraph). In T3 they don't, so answer the easiest first (Guía p.42; Manual p.19, p.28, p.30).
10. **Even if a task is above your level, try it.** If one task goes badly, the next may suit you better (Guía p.38).
11. **Practise reading fast, with a timer.** You need to find answers "con cierta rapidez" (Guía p.39).
12. **Expect different varieties of Spanish.** T1–T3 are as neutral as possible. T4–T5 can come from any Spanish-speaking country (Guía p.18, p.30; Edelsa).

> **Remember:** Edelsa (*Sugerencias*) says you can use *Anterior/Siguiente* "dentro de los ejercicios de las cinco tareas". The Manual and Guía are more precise: only T1 and T4 have several screens (Guía p.37; Manual p.19). Follow the Manual.

## Words & phrases that get points

**1. Notices and ads (T1)**

| Español | English | When / why |
|---|---|---|
| Se alquila / Se vende / Se busca | For rent / For sale / Wanted | The headline gives you the purpose: *"El anuncio es para personas que quieren…"* |
| Entrada libre / gratuita / gratis | Free entry | Price questions. Who exactly is free? |
| para mayores de 16 / menores de 12 / a partir de 6 años | over 16 / under 12 / from age 6 | Age traps (Modelo 0, T1-4) |
| Horario: de lunes a viernes, festivos, fin de semana | Opening hours… | Compare the lines: "different on weekends" |
| Venta de entradas en… / Compra tu entrada en… | Tickets sold at… | Where you buy ≠ where it happens |
| Plazas limitadas, inscripción, reserva previa | Limited places, sign-up, booking required | Obligation questions |
| Es obligatorio / Prohibido / No se permite | Required / Forbidden | "tiene que" vs. "puede" |
| Rebajas, descuento, oferta, más barato que | Sales, discount… | "barato" paraphrases |

**2. Emails and messages (T1–T2)**

| Español | English | When / why |
|---|---|---|
| Te escribo para… / Le escribo porque… | I'm writing to… | Answers the "¿para qué?" question |
| ¿Te apetece…? / ¿Quieres venir…? / ¿Qué te parece si…? | Do you fancy…? / How about…? | Option says *invitar, proponer* |
| Perdona… / Siento no… | Sorry… | Usually small talk, not the purpose |
| Por cierto… / ¿Sabes con quién me encontré? | By the way… | New topic, often another **person** (wrong-person trap) |
| Dime algo / Escríbeme pronto / Hablamos | Let me know | Close of the email; asks for an answer |
| Querido/a, Un beso, Un abrazo vs. Estimado/a, Un saludo, Atentamente | informal vs. formal | Shows the relationship (a general question) |

**3. Small words that flip the answer (T1–T3)**

| Español | English | When / why |
|---|---|---|
| pero / sin embargo / aunque | but / however / although | What comes after usually decides the answer |
| pensaba / iba a + infinitivo… pero | I was going to… but | A plan that changed (Modelo 0, T3-8) |
| al principio… al final | at first… in the end | A change over time |
| ya / ya no / todavía (no) | already / no longer / still (not) | Present vs. past situation |
| solo, excepto, salvo, ni… ni, nunca, nadie | only, except, neither… nor, never | Restriction and negation |
| volver a + inf. / dejar de + inf. / seguir + gerundio | do again / stop doing / keep doing | "repetiría", "ha dejado de" (Manual p.29) |
| había + participio | had done | B1 pluperfect: what happened *earlier* (Guía p.40) |
| se lo / me lo / les | to him/her/them | Who did what to whom (the "aconsejar" trap) |

**4. Paraphrase pairs to recognise** (text → correct option; Guía p.155–158)

| Text says | Option says | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| canta con … y otros amigos | hay diferentes cantantes | several singers |
| al lado del metro y de paradas de autobús | bien comunicada | good transport links |
| empieza a las 22:00 | es por la noche | at night |
| lo pasó muy bien | estuvo muy contento | had a great time |
| quería escapar del día a día | buscaba un cambio en su vida | wanted a change |
| aprendí a organizar mi economía | aprendió a administrar su dinero | managing money |
| me gustaría volver algún día | repetiría la experiencia | would do it again |

**5. Same thing, different countries** (SIELE uses all varieties; even the Manual's T1 practice uses *computadora*, p.23)

| Spain | Latin America | English |
|---|---|---|
| ordenador | computadora | computer |
| móvil | celular | mobile phone |
| coche | carro / auto | car |
| billete | boleto | ticket |
| piso | departamento / apartamento | flat |
| zumo | jugo | juice |

### Tarea 2 (A2) – Recommended time: 12 minutes

*Usted va a leer un correo que Javier ha escrito a su amigo Tomás. Elija la opción correcta para cada una de las cinco preguntas.*

> Hola, Tomás:
>
> ¿Qué tal todo? Perdona por no contestar antes a tu mensaje, pero estas dos semanas han sido muy complicadas. Como sabes, el mes pasado dejé mi trabajo en el banco y ahora trabajo en una agencia de viajes del centro. Me gusta mucho más, aunque al principio estaba un poco nervioso porque no conocía a nadie. Ahora estoy muy contento: mis compañeros son muy simpáticos y mi jefa, Ana, me ayuda mucho.
>
> El sábado pasado fuimos todos a celebrar el cumpleaños de Ana en un restaurante mexicano. La comida estaba buenísima, pero el restaurante estaba muy lejos y volví a casa en taxi a las dos de la mañana. ¡Me costó cuarenta euros!
>
> Pero te escribo, sobre todo, por otra cosa. Mi hermana Elena se casa el 12 de julio en Sevilla y quiere invitarte a la boda. Ya sabes que Elena te conoce desde que íbamos al colegio y siempre le has caído muy bien. La ceremonia es a las seis de la tarde en una iglesia del barrio de Triana y después hay una cena en un hotel junto al río. Elena necesita saber antes del 30 de mayo si vas a ir, porque tiene que organizar las mesas.
>
> Si vienes, puedes dormir en casa de mis padres: tienen una habitación libre y está muy cerca del hotel. Yo voy a llegar a Sevilla dos días antes para ayudar a mi hermana con las flores y la música. Si quieres, podemos ir juntos en tren desde Madrid; el viaje solo dura dos horas y media.
>
> Escríbeme pronto y dime algo.
>
> Un abrazo,\

## Answer key

**Tarea 1**
1. **c**: "¿Vamos el jueves…?" means changing the day. (a) the hospital is an echo; (b) a restaurant is mentioned, but there is no booking.
2. **b**: Sundays are 10:00–14:00 only, so it's closed in the afternoon. (a) the classes are for over-65s; (c) "gratis" refers to the classes, not to adults on Mondays.
3. **a**: "tiene dos años" means a used bike. (b) is Mario's own reason; (c) the helmet is a gift, and "tienda" is only used to compare the price.
4. **c**: you buy the ticket at the library before Thursday, which is before Saturday. (a) is a place swap; (b) "comida típica" belongs to the market.
5. **b**: no hot water from 9:00 to 13:00. (a) is a contradiction (the lift works); (c) "puede" is an option, not an obligation.

**Tarea 2**
1. **b**: "te escribo, sobre todo, por otra cosa" introduces Elena's wedding invitation, in paragraph 3 and not paragraph 1. (a) the birthday is Ana's and already past; (c) Ana is the one who helps him.
2. **c**: "mis compañeros son muy simpáticos". (a) Ana is the boss (wrong person); (b) he was nervous (contradiction).
3. **a**: "a las dos de la mañana". (b) he ate at a Mexican restaurant; he didn't cook. (c) it was Ana's birthday (wrong person).
4. **b**: "podemos ir juntos en tren". (a) the hotel is where the dinner is; he offers his parents' house. (c) helping with the flowers is Javier's job.
5. **c**: "te conoce desde que íbamos al colegio". (a) they'd travel *from Madrid*, and he'd need a bed in Sevilla; (b) the travel agency is Javier's job (wrong person).

**Tarea 3**
1. **a (Rosa)**: "pensé seriamente en volver a casa" in the first days. Daniel's "al principio" is about not talking to people.
2. **c (Isabel)**: her children "nos dijeron que era una experiencia que no nos podíamos perder". Daniel uses the echo "Decidí hacer el Camino" but nobody advised him; Rosa's friends encouraged her to *continue*, not to start.
3. **b (Daniel)**: he carried bread, fruit and cheese and almost never ate in restaurants. Rosa's "baratos" is about the hostels.
4. **a (Rosa)**: "con un chico italiano todavía hablo". Daniel met people but says nothing about keeping in touch.
5. **c (Isabel)**: the rain made them stay in a village. Rosa found the heat hard but never stopped (says something similar).
6. **b (Daniel)**: "con una vez tengo suficiente". Rosa says the opposite ("seguro que lo haré").
7. **c (Isabel)**: the luggage service "no es barato, pero… merece la pena".
8. **b (Daniel)**: he came back with clear ideas and now has his own company. "Empresa" in Isabel's text and "trabajo" in Rosa's are echo traps.

The answers split 2–3–3. They are not balanced, just like Modelo 0.

**Tarea 4 mini-set:** 1 **b** ("En ellos" = the urban gardens) · 2 **d** (benefits beyond money; "Además" then adds another one) · 3 **a** ("Esto" = the polluted soil; "Por eso" then follows) · 4 **e** ("esta demanda" = the waiting lists). **c** is the extra fragment: "Por el contrario" contradicts the text's message of success.
