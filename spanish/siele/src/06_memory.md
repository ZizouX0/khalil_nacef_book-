# 🧠 Kit de memoria · Memory kit

**The night before and the morning of the exam:** read the four one-page cheat sheets. Nothing new.

**On the bus:** cut out the flashcards, fold them in half, and test yourself. Read the Spanish side, say the meaning, then flip.

## 📖 Reading on one page

:::stats
60 min | 5 tasks, 38 questions, one clock
18 | correct answers = B1 (10 = A2)
5 + 5 + 8 | items in Tareas 1–3: your 18
0 | points lost for a wrong answer
:::

:::golden The 4 rules
1. **Same words = suspicious.** The right option says it in other words: "al lado del metro" → *bien comunicada*.
2. **Siguiente = exit.** There is no way back. Only T1 and T4 have more than one screen.
3. **Leave Tarea 3 at minute 42.** Plan 10 / 12 / 20 min for T1–T3, then put an answer in every gap of T4–T5.
4. **Mouse only, no blanks.** The keyboard can change your answers. A guess costs nothing.
:::

:::trap Echo word · Tarea 1
"el lunes" is the day the tickets go on sale, not the day of the concert (Guía p.155).
**Fix:** ask "does the text say this about *this* thing?"
:::

:::trap Wrong person, wrong time · Tarea 2
In Modelo 0, all 10 wrong options were about another person, another time or another place (Guía p.157).
**Fix:** check **who** and **when** in every option.
:::

:::trap Something similar · Tarea 3
*Se lo aconsejo a todo el mundo* (she gives advice) ≠ *se lo aconsejaron* (friends advised her) (Guía p.158).
**Fix:** for each question, sort the 3 texts: gives it / says something similar / doesn't mention it.
:::

:::remember
T1 and T2 follow the order of the text. T3 doesn't, so do the easy questions first.
T3 answers are not shared out evenly (4–2–2 in Modelo 0).
The email's real purpose is often not in paragraph 1.
:::

## 🎧 Listening on one page

:::stats
55 min | 6 tasks, 38 questions
2× | every audio plays twice, automatically
18 → 22 | your mock score → a safe B1
8 | Tarea 3 items: your extra points are here
:::

:::golden The 4 rules
1. **Use the 30 seconds.** Before T1–T3, read every option and predict the words you will hear.
2. **Same idea, other words.** The word you hear is often the trap.
3. **Decide now.** In T2 and T3, answer after each audio. Later audios won't help.
4. **Click an answer for every item.** The task moves on 30 s after the second play. Never press *Siguiente* early.
:::

:::flow Tarea 3 in five beats
30 s | read the 11 sentences, tag each one in 1–2 words, bracket the twins
Play 1 | WHO is speaking + the TURN word + the VERDICT; write 3 key words
5 s | look at the twin of each candidate
Play 2 | listen only for the difference: who? did it really happen? when?
10 s | click, cross the letter out, move on
:::

:::trap The opening isn't the point
"Yo me lo pasé muy bien…" The answer is *Llovió mucho* (Guía p.170).
**Fix:** wait for the end of the story.
:::

:::trap The turn after *pero*
"Lo pasamos bien, sí, pero lo mejor fue el viaje…" → *Disfrutó de la luna de miel* (Guía p.171).
**Fix:** the sentence after *pero / lo mejor fue / al final* is gold.
:::

:::trap Wrong person
"Mi primo llegó tarde" ≠ *El novio no llegó puntual* (Guía p.170).
**Fix:** check every *él, ella, le, mi primo*. "A él le encantó; a mí no tanto" = the speaker did **not** love it.
:::

:::remember
Never choose on one word. 3 sentences are bait: they match nobody.
Missed a person? Cross out the letters you used and guess from the rest.
:::

## ✍️ Writing on one page

:::stats
50 min | 2 tasks, one clock: you can go back
100–150 | words in Tarea 1 = half the test
34 % | Cumplimiento: every pauta counts
104 | points = B1 (band 3 everywhere = 104.5)
:::

:::golden The 4 rules
1. **Every pauta, every sub-point.** "*qué le gusta y qué no*" = 2 sub-points.
2. **Pauta 4 = a real story in the past.** When, who, what happened, how it felt.
3. **Link on purpose.** 4+ different B1 connectors, 1–2 relatives (*que, donde*), *creo que*.
4. **Tarea 1 first (≈ 30 min), then a short Tarea 2 (≈ 15 min).** Tarea 2 can only add points. Last 3–5 min: check Tarea 1 again.
:::

:::model Tarea 1 skeleton · 5 pautas = 5 paragraphs
**P1 · saludar:** ¡Hola, Nuria! ==¡Qué alegría recibir tu correo!==

**P2 · informar:** Te cuento: ahora vivo en …, una zona ==donde== hay … . ==Además==, … .

**P3 · describir, gustos:** ==Lo que más me gusta== es … ==porque== … . Lo que menos, …, ==aunque== … .

**P4 · contar:** ==El verano pasado fui== a … con … . ==Hacía== … y, ==de repente==, … . ==Al final==, … . ¡Fue inolvidable!

**P5 · despedirse:** ==Espero que nos veamos== pronto. Un abrazo, Sam

---

- *Indefinido* for the events + *imperfecto* for the background = "narrar historias", band 3.
- Fill it with **your own** content. Memorised sentences are marked down (Manual p.72).
:::

:::trap Mixed register
"¿Cómo estás, tío?" … "Un cordial saludo" (Manual p.76).
**Fix:** *tú* or *usted* from the first line to the last.
:::

:::trap Answering a different question
Asked about "years abroad", a candidate wrote about "last month in Granada" and lost pauta 4 (Guía p.95).
**Fix:** read the verb: *contar* = a story in the past.
:::

:::trap A bad ending
Ending with *¿Cómo estás?*, or with no farewell at all (Guía p.102).
**Fix:** a wish + a farewell: *Espero que… Un abrazo.*
:::

:::remember
Number the pautas on your paper. Tick each one when it is in your text.
No spellchecker: type ¿ ¡ á ñ with the *Teclado español* button.
:::

## 🗣️ Speaking on one page

:::stats
5 tasks | 15–20 minutes, all recorded
50 | points per task (20 % each)
125 | points = B1 (76 = A2)
40 | points from band 2 in Tareas 4 + 5
:::

:::golden The 4 rules
1. **Tick every bullet.** Cumplimiento = 34 % of every task. A missing point costs more than a small mistake.
2. **Answer in the tense you hear.** *¿Cómo era…?* → *era*. *¿Qué va a hacer…?* → *voy a…*
3. **Choose fast.** 30 s for a photo or topic, 1 min in T3, or the computer picks. Your T4 topic = your T5 topic.
4. **Never silent, never skip.** Do T4 and T5. Finish your sentence before the recorder stops.
:::

:::flow Tarea 1 · always the same shape
Q1 · 15 s | present + 1 detail: *Soy… Nací en…, pero vivo en…*
Q2 · 15 s | present, a full sentence: never just *sí* or *no*
Q3 · 30 s | past: *Cuando era pequeño, vivía…* or *Este fin de semana he ido…*
Q4 · 30 s | future: *Voy a… porque… También me gustaría…*
:::

:::icons The 5 photo points · Tarea 2 · all 5 by 1:30
👥 | 1 · Cuántas personas hay y qué relación tienen
👕 | 2 · Cómo son y qué ropa llevan
🏃 | 3 · Qué están haciendo y cómo se sienten
🏠 | 4 · En qué lugar están y cómo es
🍽️ | 5 · Qué objetos hay, cómo son y dónde están
:::

:::trap Forgetting points 4–5
The official sample skipped the place and the objects: Cumplimiento band 3 (Guía p.136).
**Fix:** speak in the order 1 → 5, then add extras.
:::

:::trap A late replay · Tarea 1
Replaying the question after the recording starts eats your seconds (Guía p.143).
**Fix:** replay it **before** you speak.
:::

:::trap A missing bullet · Tarea 3
The sample never said what the car was for: band 4, not 5 (Guía p.137).
**Fix:** in the 20 s, find one idea per bullet and decide *tú* or *usted*.
:::

:::remember
Every point = points. Silence = zero.
:::

## 🧩 The memory tricks

Say them aloud until they come out without thinking.

### Reading & Listening

:::big
Same words = suspicious.
Same meaning = correct.
:::

Listening signal words: dig the answer out with your **PALAS** (shovels).

:::mnemonic PALAS
P | pero | the point comes right after it
A | aunque | the exception is the point
L | lo mejor fue · lo que sí | a spotlight on the answer
A | al final | the outcome, often the answer
S | sin embargo | a formal *pero*: same job
:::

On play 1 of each person in Tarea 3, listen for three things:

:::mnemonic WHO · TURN · VERDICT
👤 | WHO | man or woman? bride, guest, boss? Who did it?
↪️ | TURN | *pero, en realidad, al final, lo mejor fue*
⚖️ | VERDICT | good, bad, a surprise or a change?
:::

### Writing

The 5 pautas, in order, in one silly sentence. Each word is one step up the staircase.

:::mnemonic Hi, I like stories. Bye!
1 | Hi | saludar, and react to their message
2 | I | Information: *decir, informar* [A1]
3 | like | *describir*, likes and dislikes [A2]
4 | stories | *contar*: a story in the past [B1]
5 | Bye | *despedirse*: a wish + a farewell
:::

Revise in the order of the 4 Cs (Guía p.113). Then do one last pass only for á é í ó ú ñ ¿ ¡.

:::mnemonic 4 C
C | Check | Cumplimiento: every pauta and sub-point, a greeting and a farewell
C | Connect | Cohesión: paragraphs, connectors, *que / donde*
C | Correct | Corrección: tenses, agreement, *ser / estar*, *gusta / gustan*
C | Colour | Alcance: swap a repeated *bonito* for *precioso, animado, tranquilo*
:::

:::big
A2 lists.
B1 links, judges and tells a story.
:::

### Speaking

Tarea 1 always has the same rhythm:

:::mnemonic HOY · HOY · AYER · MAÑANA
1 | Hoy | Q1: present, 15 s
2 | Hoy | Q2: present, 15 s
3 | Ayer | Q3: past, 30 s: *era, tenía* or *he ido, fui*
4 | Mañana | Q4: future, 30 s: *voy a…, me gustaría…*
:::

The photo: look through the **GLASS** (a magnifying glass), point by point, and count the five on your fingers.

:::mnemonic GLASS
G | Gente | how many people, and how they are related
L | Look | what they look like and what they wear
A | Acción | what they are doing (*están + -ando*) and how they feel
S | Sitio | where they are and what the place is like
S | Stuff | the objects: what they are like and where they are
:::

Tarea 3 is a voice message. Build it like a sandwich, and use the same bread top and bottom: *tú* or *usted* all the way.

:::mnemonic SÁNDWICH
🍞 | Hello + why you call | *¡Hola, Marta! Te llamo porque…* / *Buenos días. Le llamo porque…*
🥬 | Bullet 1 | apologise, invite, say what you need: *Lo siento mucho, pero…*
🍅 | Bullet 2 | reasons and details: *Es que… / Como…, …*
🧀 | Bullet 3 | propose or ask: *¿Qué te parece si…? / ¿Sería posible…?*
🍞 | Closing | *Llámame y me dices algo.* / *Muchas gracias por su ayuda.*
:::

In the photo and everywhere else, three verbs, three jobs:

:::mnemonic HAY · ESTÁ · ES
H | Hay | presents it: *hay una mesa*
E | Está | places it, or gives a mood: *está a la derecha · está cansado*
E | Es | describes it: *es de madera*
:::

:::big
Never blank in Reading and Listening.
Never silent in Speaking.
:::

### Grammar

Perfecto, indefinido or imperfecto? Ask: is the time box open, closed, or is it the scenery?

:::mnemonic HE · FUI · ERA
🔓 | HE comido | *hoy, esta semana, este año, nunca*: the time is still open
🔒 | FUI, comí, estuve | *ayer, el verano pasado, una semana*: the time is closed
🎨 | ERA, tenía, hacía | *de pequeño*, the weather, habits: the scenery of the story
:::

*El verano pasado ==fui== a la playa. ==Hacía== sol y, de repente, ==empezó== la música.*

The subjunctive is for things that are still waiting to happen. So: **WAIT**.

:::mnemonic WAIT
W | Wish | *Espero que estés bien.*
A | Ask, advise | *Quiero que vengas. Te recomiendo que visites…*
I | Important | *Es importante que reserves pronto.*
T | Time to come | *Cuando llegues, llámame.* (cuando + a future idea)
:::

The subjunctive is a band-4 bonus (Manual p.139). Not sure? The infinitive is safe: *Te recomiendo visitar…*

## 🃏 Flashcards

Cut along the lines and fold each card in half. Spanish side up: say the meaning, flip, check. Wrong? Put it back in the pile.

:::flashcards 1 · Connectors & organisers
y · pero · porque · por eso | and · but · because · so. A2 level: needed, but not enough
primero · luego · después | first · then · afterwards. Tell things in order
además · también | what's more · also. Add a second idea (B1)
aunque | although. Contrast: *Aunque llovía, salimos.*
sin embargo | however. A more formal *pero* (B1)
así que | so. A result: *Estaba cansado, así que me quedé en casa.*
Como…, … | Since… A cause. Always at the START of the sentence
donde · que · cuando | where · that · when. Join sentences: *un barrio donde hay…*
creo que… | I think that… Your opinion, on the B1 list
de repente · al final | suddenly · in the end. The two hinges of a B1 story (pauta 4)
a pesar de (que) | despite. + noun: *a pesar del frío*; + *que* + a full sentence. Band-4 bonus
en primer lugar · en conclusión | firstly · to conclude. For Writing T2 and Speaking T5
:::

:::flashcards 2 · B1 power phrases (Writing + Speaking)
Te escribo para… | I'm writing to… The first line of any message
¡Qué bien que vengas! | Great that you're coming! Pauta 1: a reaction + subjunctive
Lo que más me gusta es… porque… | What I like most is… because… Likes + a reason
Te recomiendo que visites… | I recommend you visit… Advice + subjunctive
Yo en tu lugar iría… | If I were you, I'd go… Advice with the conditional
Me lo pasé genial. · Fue inolvidable. | I had a great time. · It was unforgettable. The verdict that ends your story
Espero que nos veamos pronto. | I hope we see each other soon. Pauta 5 + subjunctive
Es que… · El problema es que… | It's just that… · The problem is that… Speaking T3: give the reason
¿Qué te parece si…? | How about…? Propose a plan to a friend (tú)
¿Sería posible…? · ¿Podría…? | Would it be possible…? · Could you…? A polite request (usted)
Creo que son… porque… · Parece que… | I think they are… because… · It seems… Photo: relationship and feelings
Pues… · A ver… · La verdad es que… | Well… · Let's see… · The truth is… Spanish fillers: never *euh*
:::

:::flashcards 3 · Listening signal words & traps
pero… | but. The point usually comes right after it
aunque… | although. The exception is the point
al final | in the end. The outcome is often the answer
en realidad | actually. It corrects what the speaker expected
lo mejor fue · lo que sí | the best part was · what I DID like. A spotlight on the answer
antes… ahora | before… now. A change: the second state counts
en cambio | on the other hand. Switches to another person or place
menos mal que · a pesar de todo | luckily · despite everything. A consolation: the verdict stays
A él le encantó; a mí no. | He loved it; I didn't. Only the speaker's view counts
¿Vas en coche? — No, … | Do you go by car? No… The reply is the answer, not the question
a las diez… no, perdona, a las diez y media | at ten… sorry, half past ten. Self-correction: the last version wins
hasta el martes ≠ a partir del martes | until Tuesday ≠ from Tuesday. Closing vs opening: check number + verb
:::

:::flashcards 4 · Grammar triggers
ser | What it is like: *Mi piso es pequeño. Es de madera.*
estar | Where it is, or a temporary state: *está en el centro · hoy está muy ruidoso*
hay | There is: presents something new: *hay un súper al lado*
hoy · este año · nunca → he + -ado | Time not finished = perfecto: *Hoy he comido con Ana.*
ayer · el verano pasado → fui, estuve | Time closed = indefinido: *Estuve una semana en Valencia.*
de pequeño → era, tenía, hacía | Habits and background = imperfecto: *Cuando era pequeño, vivía…*
Espero que… | I hope… + subjunctive: *Espero que te guste.*
Quiero que… | I want (you to)… + subjunctive: *Quiero que vengas.*
Es importante que… | It's important that… + subjunctive: *Es importante que reserves pronto.*
Cuando llegues… | When you arrive (not yet) = cuando + subjunctive: *Cuando llegues, llámame.*
por | Cause, thanks: *Gracias por tu correo.*
para | Purpose, "in order to": *Te escribo para invitarte.*
:::

## ☀️ The morning of the exam

:::checklist Tick before you leave, and in the room
- [ ] Breakfast eaten; water and a snack packed. The Global lasts about 3 hours (Manual p.14).
- [ ] The ID you registered with, plus your exam email and password.
- [ ] Leave early: be there 20 minutes before the start.
- [ ] Warm up for 10 min: 5 min of Spanish audio, then say your four Tarea 1 answers aloud (hoy · hoy · ayer · mañana).
- [ ] Reread this kit's four one-page cheat sheets. Nothing new.
- [ ] Sound check and recording check. Something wrong? Click **No** and the staff will help.
- [ ] Mouse only, no dictionary. The 2 sheets and the pen are for notes.
- [ ] Reading and Listening: an answer in every item before *Siguiente*.
- [ ] Writing: Tarea 1 first, every pauta ticked, accents last. Then a short Tarea 2.
- [ ] Speaking: every bullet, the tense you hear, no silence. Do T4 and T5. Between tests, drink water and let the last test go.
:::
