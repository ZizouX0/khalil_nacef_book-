# 🧠 Kit de memoria · Memory kit

**The night before:** reread the one-page summary at the start of each test chapter, then the tricks below.

**On the bus:** cut out the flashcards, fold them in half, and test yourself. Read the Spanish side, say the meaning, then flip.

## 🧩 The memory tricks

Say them aloud until they come out without thinking.

### The whole exam

Learn your B1 code:

:::mnemonic 18 · 18 · 3 · 125
📖 | Reading | 18 correct = B1 · 10 = A2
🎧 | Listening | 18 correct = B1 · 10 = A2
✍️ | Writing | Task 1 at band 3 = B1 · band 2 = A2
🗣️ | Speaking | 125 points (half of 250) = B1 · 76 = A2
:::

:::big
Blank = 0. Guess = a free chance.
:::

### Reading

:::big
Same words = suspicious.
Same meaning = correct.
:::

:::mnemonic 6 SOSPECHOSOS
🦜 | El Loro | repeats the text's words, but in the wrong place
🎭 | El Impostor | true… but about another person
⏳ | El Viajero | true… but at another time: the past, or only a plan
🔍 | El Zoom | too big or too small: algunos → todos, puede → tiene que
🙃 | Don Contrario | the text says the opposite
👯 | El Gemelo | (T3) another person says something similar
:::

### Listening

:::big
Don't match words.
Match the story.
:::

On play 1 of each person in Tarea 3, catch three things:

:::mnemonic ¿Quién? · ¿Pero…? · ¿Qué tal?
👤 | ¿Quién? | who is speaking: man or woman, bride, guest, boss?
↩️ | ¿Pero…? | the turn: what comes after *pero, aunque, en realidad*?
⭐ | ¿Qué tal? | the verdict: good, bad, a surprise, a change?
:::

Picture 6 guests at the door. Every wrong option is one of them:

:::mnemonic GUESTS
G | Ghost | Said, but it never happened
U | U-turn | The point comes after *pero*
E | Echo | Same word, other story
S | Silver lining | A happy last line hides the verdict
T | Twins | Two sentences look alike
S | Stranger | It happened to someone else
:::

The U-turn words ↩️: dig the answer out with your **PALAS** (shovels).

:::mnemonic PALAS
P | pero | the point comes next
A | aunque | the other half counts
L | lo mejor fue · lo que sí | a spotlight on the answer
A | al final | often the answer
S | sin embargo | a formal *pero*: same job
:::

### Writing

The staircase: 👋 hello → facts → photo → film → 👋 goodbye. The three middle steps:

:::mnemonic FACTS · PHOTO · FILM
📋 | Facts | informar · *vivo en…, tengo…, hay…*
📷 | Photo | describir · *es…, está…, me gusta… porque…*
🎬 | Film | contar · *fui…, hacía…, de repente…, al final…*
:::

:::big
Pauta 4 is a film, not a list.
:::

:::big
Every *y* is a hidden pauta.
:::

Connectors are *plata* (money): each one earns points.

:::mnemonic PLATA
P | Primero | start your story
L | Luego | then (or *después*)
A | Además | add one more idea
T | También | also
A | Aunque · Así que | contrast · result
:::

Revise in the order of the 4 Cs (Guía p.113). Then one last pass only for á é í ó ú ñ ¿ ¡.

:::mnemonic 4 C
C | Check | Cumplimiento: every pauta and sub-point, hello and goodbye
C | Connect | Cohesión: paragraphs, connectors, *que / donde*
C | Correct | Corrección: tenses, agreement, *ser / estar*, *gusta / gustan*
C | Colour | Alcance: swap a repeated *bonito* for *precioso, animado, tranquilo*
:::

:::big
A2 lists. B1 links.
:::

### Speaking

:::mnemonic HOY · HOY · AYER · MAÑANA
Q1 | Hoy | a fact about you now: *Soy… Vivo en…*
Q2 | Hoy | another fact about you now: *Trabajo en… Me gusta…*
Q3 | Ayer | past: *era, tenía, he ido, fui*
Q4 | Mañana | future: *voy a…, me gustaría…*
:::

Take the photo for a *paseo* (a walk). Five letters, five fingers, five points:

:::mnemonic PASEO
P | Personas | how many, and how they are related: *Creo que son… porque…*
A | Aspecto y ropa | what they look like and wear: *es alto, lleva…*
S | Sucede y sienten | what is happening and how they feel: *Están cocinando. Parece que…*
E | En qué lugar | where they are and what it is like
O | Objetos | what there is and where: *Encima de la mesa hay…*
:::

Tarea 3 = hola → porque → 1, 2, 3 → adiós. Build it like a sandwich, with the same bread top and bottom: *tú* or *usted* all the way.

:::mnemonic SÁNDWICH
🍞 | Hola | hello and who you are: *¡Hola, Marta! Soy…* / *Buenos días. Me llamo…*
🧈 | Porque | why you call: *Te llamo porque…* / *Le llamo porque…*
🥬 | 1 | apologise, invite or give the news
🍅 | 2 | reasons and details: *Es que… / Como…, …*
🧀 | 3 | propose or ask: *¿Qué te parece si…? / ¿Sería posible…?*
🍞 | Adiós | *Llámame y me dices algo.* / *Muchas gracias por su ayuda.*
:::

Three verbs, three jobs:

:::mnemonic HAY · ESTÁ · ES
H | Hay | it exists: *hay una mesa*
E | Está | where it is, or a mood: *está a la derecha · está cansado*
E | Es | what it is like: *es de madera*
:::

:::big
Tick every point. Fill the clock. Never skip a task.
:::

### Grammar

Perfecto, indefinido or imperfecto? Ask: is the time box open, closed, or is it the scenery?

:::mnemonic HE · FUI · ERA
🔓 | HE comido | *hoy, esta semana, este año, nunca*: the time is still open
🔒 | FUI, comí, estuve | *ayer, el verano pasado, una semana*: the time is closed
🎨 | ERA, tenía, hacía | *de pequeño*, the weather, habits: the scenery of the story
:::

*El verano pasado ==fui== a la playa. ==Hacía== sol y, de repente, ==empezó== la música.*

The subjunctive is for things still waiting to happen. So: **WAIT**.

:::mnemonic WAIT
W | Wish | *Espero que estés bien.*
A | Ask, advise | *Quiero que vengas. Te recomiendo que visites…*
I | Important | *Es importante que reserves pronto.*
T | Time to come | *Cuando llegues, llámame.* (cuando + a future idea)
:::

The subjunctive is a B1 bonus. Not sure? The infinitive is safe: *Te recomiendo visitar…*

## 🃏 Flashcards

Cut along the lines and fold each card in half. Spanish side up: say the meaning, flip, check. Wrong? Put it back in the pile.

:::flashcards 1 · Connectors & organisers
y · pero · porque · por eso | and · but · because · so. A2 level: needed, but not enough
primero · luego · después | first · then · afterwards. PLATA: tell things in order
además · también | what's more · also. PLATA: add one more idea (B1)
aunque | although. Contrast: *Aunque llovía, salimos.*
sin embargo | however. A more formal *pero* (B1)
así que | so. A result: *Estaba cansado, así que me quedé en casa.*
Como…, … | Since… A cause. Always at the START of the sentence
donde · que · cuando | where · that · when. Join sentences: *un barrio donde hay…*
creo que… | I think that… Your opinion, on the B1 list
de repente · al final | suddenly · in the end. The two hinges of your film (pauta 4)
a pesar de (que) | despite. + noun: *a pesar del frío*; + *que* + a full sentence. B2 bonus
en primer lugar · en conclusión | firstly · to conclude. For Writing T2 and Speaking T5
:::

:::flashcards 2 · B1 power phrases (Writing + Speaking)
Te escribo para… | I'm writing to… The first line of any message
¡Qué bien que vengas! | Great that you're coming! The hello step + subjunctive
Lo que más me gusta es… porque… | What I like most is… because… The photo step: likes + a reason
Te recomiendo que visites… | I recommend you visit… Advice + subjunctive (WAIT · A)
Yo en tu lugar iría… | If I were you, I'd go… Advice with the conditional
Me lo pasé genial. · Fue inolvidable. | I had a great time. · It was unforgettable. The last frame of your film
Espero que nos veamos pronto. | I hope we see each other soon. The goodbye step + subjunctive
Es que… · El problema es que… | It's just that… · The problem is that… Speaking T3, bullet 2: the reason
¿Qué te parece si…? | How about…? Speaking T3, bullet 3, to a friend (tú)
¿Sería posible…? · ¿Podría…? | Would it be possible…? · Could you…? Bullet 3, polite (usted)
Creo que son… porque… · Parece que… | I think they are… because… · It seems… PASEO: P and S
Pues… · A ver… · La verdad es que… | Well… · Let's see… · The truth is… Spanish fillers: never *euh*
:::

:::flashcards 3 · Listening signal words & traps
pero… | but. ↩️ U-turn: the point comes next
aunque… | although. The other half counts
al final | in the end. Often the answer
en realidad | actually. ↩️ It corrects the expectation
lo mejor fue · lo que sí | the best part · what I DID like. A spotlight on the answer
antes… ahora | before… now. "Now" counts
no… sino… | not… but… The *sino* part is true
menos mal que · a pesar de todo | luckily · despite it all. 🩹 Silver lining: the verdict stays
A él le encantó; a mí no. | He loved it; I didn't. 👤 Stranger: only the speaker counts
¿Vas en coche? — No, … | Do you go by car? No… 👻 Ghost: the reply is the answer
a las diez… no, perdona, a las diez y media | at ten… sorry, half past ten. ↩️ U-turn: the last word wins
hasta el martes ≠ a partir del martes | until Tuesday ≠ from Tuesday. 👻 Ghost: closes vs opens
:::

:::flashcards 4 · Grammar triggers
ser | What it is like: *Mi piso es pequeño. Es de madera.*
estar | Where it is, or a temporary state: *está en el centro · hoy está muy ruidoso*
hay | It exists: presents something new: *hay un súper al lado*
hoy · este año · nunca → he + -ado | 🔓 Time still open = perfecto: *Hoy he comido con Ana.*
ayer · el verano pasado → fui, estuve | 🔒 Time closed = indefinido: *Estuve una semana en Valencia.*
de pequeño → era, tenía, hacía | 🎨 Habits and scenery = imperfecto: *Cuando era pequeño, vivía…*
Espero que… | I hope… + subjunctive (WAIT · W): *Espero que te guste.*
Quiero que… | I want (you to)… + subjunctive (WAIT · A): *Quiero que vengas.*
Es importante que… | It's important that… + subjunctive (WAIT · I): *Es importante que reserves pronto.*
Cuando llegues… | When you arrive (not yet) = cuando + subjunctive (WAIT · T): *Cuando llegues, llámame.*
por | Cause, thanks: *Gracias por tu correo.*
para | Purpose, "in order to": *Te escribo para invitarte.*
:::
