## Tarea 1 · A1 · Una conversación, cinco huecos

A man and a woman chat about daily life. Fill **5 sentences** from **15 options** (a)–(ñ). The facts are said clearly (Guía p.47).

:::flow Tarea 1 · the clock
30 s | read sentences + options
Play 1 | whole conversation
10 s | fill the sure ones
Play 2 | whole conversation again
30 s | finish and check
:::

**The secret: 15 options = 5 families of 3** (Guía p.75). Modelo 0: *abuela/hermana/madre*, *primer/segundo/tercer*… All 15 are said in the audio: 10 about something else.

:::steps Your routine
1. **Sort** the 15 options into 5 families.
2. **Read the gap's grammar:** *en el ___ piso* needs *primer/segundo/tercer*.
3. **Label** each sentence with its family: 3 candidates left.
4. **Play 1:** pick the sure ones. **Play 2:** complete (Manual p.47).
:::

:::trap 👤 The Stranger — about whom?
*Sara dice que el amigo de Mateo es ___*: Sara says *tímido*, Mateo adds *simpático*. Only *tímido* counts (Manual p.46).
**Fix:** ask "who says it, who does it?"
:::

:::trap 👻 The Ghost — question, then "No"
*«¿Vas en coche? — No, siempre voy en autobús.»* The **reply** is the answer.
:::

:::trap ↩️ The U-turn — the last word wins
*«a las diez… no, perdona, a las diez y media»*. A customer who says *«Bueno, no me gusta mucho…»* buys the **second** book.
:::

:::remember
Find the family of 3.
Then catch the right sibling.
:::

📚 **Practise:** Manual pp.44–47 · Guía p.162 (transcript p.168, solutions p.183) · Ejercicio 1.

## Tarea 2 · A2 · Cinco anuncios de radio

5 short ads or news items, one question each, a b c. **The instruction never changes** (Guía p.49). 2 questions ask the general idea, 3 ask a detail.

:::flow Tarea 2 · the clock
30 s | read the 5 questions
Ad 1 · play 1 | listen for question 1 only
5 s | pause
Ad 1 · play 2 | check the detail
10 s | «Conteste a la pregunta número 1» → click
Ads 2–5 | same loop, then 30 s check
:::

:::icons Five questions for every ad (Manual p.50)
📣 | ¿Qué anuncia?
👥 | ¿A quién se dirige?
📍 | ¿Dónde es?
🕐 | ¿Cuándo es?
💶 | ¿Cuánto cuesta?
:::

:::steps Your routine
1. **One ad, one question.** Listen only for it (Manual p.49).
2. **Check on play 2:** the number + its noun + the verb.
3. **Click and forget.** Later ads won't help (Guía p.75).
:::

:::trap 🔁 The Echo — background scenery
The book ad mentions Egypt, Venice and cinema… but it sells **a novel** (Guía p.169).
**Fix:** ask "what is it **selling**?" Check each number **with its noun**.
:::

:::trap 👻 The Ghost — the opposite
*«abierta hasta el martes»* ≠ *se abre el martes*. *«muebles clásicos»* rules out *modernos* (Guía p.169).
**Fix:** listen for *hasta / a partir de*, *no, sin*.
:::

:::tip The right answer wears a disguise
*precios baratos* → *precios más bajos* · *novela* → *libro* · *entre 17 y 20 años* → *para jóvenes* (Guía p.169).
:::

:::remember
One ad, one question.
Click it, forget it.
:::

📚 **Practise:** Manual pp.48–51 · Guía p.163 (transcript p.169, solutions p.183) · Ejercicio 2.

## ⭐ Tarea 3 · B1 · Ocho personas, un tema

**Your priority:** 8 points, the biggest block at your level.

- **8 people** (4 men, 4 women) tell a short personal story on **one topic**, 50–70 words each (Manual p.52).
- **11 sentences** (a)–(k). 8 are used. **3 match nobody.**
- They test the **main point**. It is said clearly: no hidden meanings (Guía p.57).

Instruction: *«Usted va a escuchar a ocho personas que hablan sobre ==[el tema]==. Elija la frase que corresponde a cada persona…»* Only the topic changes.

:::levelup From T2 to T3
**T2 · A2** · one ad, one detail, present tense.
*El museo abre de martes a domingo.*
---
**T3 · B1** · one story, one point, ==past tenses==, ==pero / aunque==.
*Cuando llegué, ==ya había empezado==, ==pero al final== lo pasé bien.*
:::

:::flow Tarea 3 · the clock (about 10 min)
30 s | read the 11 sentences: tag them, bracket the twins
Persona 1 · play 1 | eyes off the screen: ¿Quién? ¿Pero…? ¿Qué tal?
5 s | look at your 2 candidates
Persona 1 · play 2 | listen only for the difference
10 s | click, write the letter, cross it out
Personas 2–8 | the same loop × 7
Final 30 s | 8 filled, no letter twice
:::

(Guía p.53; the 10 min is my estimate)

### Before Persona 1: tag the 11 sentences

:::icons 4 kinds of sentence (Guía p.164)
🌧️ | **Event** — *Llovió mucho. Llegó con retraso.*
⭐ | **Verdict** — *Fue un éxito. Resultó un desastre.*
👥 | **People** — *Conoció a alguien.*
📍 | **Place** — *Se celebró en un barco.*
:::

:::steps The 30 seconds
1. **Note the topic.** What can go well or badly?
2. **Tag** each sentence in 1–2 words: *a-boat, b-countries…*
3. **Bracket the twins:** *Llegó con retraso* / *El espectáculo empezó tarde*.
4. **Draw a column:** P1 __ P2 __ … P8 __.
:::

### The loop: play 1, play 2

Play 1, catch 3 things: **¿Quién?** (who speaks) · **¿Pero…?** (the turn) · **¿Qué tal?** (the verdict).

:::steps For each person
1. **Play 1 — listen, don't read.** ¿Quién? ¿Pero…? ¿Qué tal?
2. **Write 3 key words** ("rain after church") + 1–2 candidate letters.
3. **5 s — compare** each candidate with its twin.
4. **Play 2 — the difference only.** Who? Did it really happen? That day or later?
5. **10 s — click, cross out, move on.** Later people won't help (Guía p.76).
:::

:::checklist Final 30 seconds
- 8 menus filled, no letter twice.
- The 3 left-overs feel "never said".
- Missed one? Only 3–4 letters are left. Guess.
:::

:::big
Don't match words.
Match the story.
:::

### The trap families: check the GUESTS

Modelo 0 was about a wedding. Picture **6 guests** at the door: every wrong option is one of them.

:::mnemonic GUESTS
G | Ghost | Said, but it never happened
U | U-turn | The point comes after *pero*
E | Echo | Same word, other story
S | Silver lining | A happy last line hides the verdict
T | Twins | Two sentences look alike
S | Stranger | It happened to someone else
:::

:::trap 👻 G · The Ghost — said, never happened
Denied, cancelled, feared or only planned.
*«el grupo de música que habíamos contratado ==no fue==»* → ✗ *Hubo una orquesta* (Guía p.170)
**Fix:** hunt for *no, ni, sin, iba a, pensaba que*. Did it **really** happen?
:::

:::trap ↩️ U · The U-turn — after *pero*
Sentence 1 is a warm-up. The answer comes **after the turn**.
*«Yo me lo pasé muy bien»*… then rain after the church → ✓ *Llovió mucho*, ✗ *Fue un éxito*.
*«Lo pasamos bien, sí, ==pero lo mejor fue== el viaje… por el Caribe»* → ✓ *Disfrutó de la luna de miel* (Guía p.171)
**Fix:** never click on sentence 1. Wait for *pero, aunque, en realidad, antes… ahora*.
:::

:::trap 🔁 E · The Echo — same word, other story
*«salí de casa ==un poco tarde==… me encontré una fiesta»* → ✓ *La fiesta se celebró en su lugar de trabajo*, ✗ *No llegó a tiempo* (Manual p.54)
Lists and numbers are decoration.
**Fix:** the right answer wears a **disguise**: *llevábamos un año casados* → *aniversario de boda*.
:::

:::trap 🩹 S · The Silver Lining — a happy last line
*«…y no por un buen motivo… A pesar de todo, me alegro»* → ✓ *Resultó un desastre* (Guía p.170)
And a scare that ends well (*menos mal que…*) is not a disaster.
**Fix:** judge the day or the trip, not life in general.
:::

:::trap 👯 T · The Twins — two sentences look alike
*Llegó con retraso* / *El espectáculo empezó tarde* (Manual p.55)
*Tuvo lugar en el campo* / *Fue en su pueblo*
**Fix:** on play 2 ask **who**, **what** exactly, **when**.
:::

:::trap 👤 S · The Stranger — someone else's story
*«==Mi primo== llegó tarde»* → ✗ *El novio no llegó puntual* (Guía p.170)
*«A él le encantó; ==a mí no tanto==»* → the speaker did **not** love it.
**Fix:** check every *él, ella, le, mi hermana*. The screen is about **the speaker**.
:::

:::remember
Check the GUESTS before you click.
:::

### Signal words: the answer is near

:::phrases The turn words
pero | but | ↩️ the point comes next
aunque | although | the other half counts
en realidad | actually | corrects the expectation
al final | in the end | often the answer
lo que sí · lo mejor fue | what I did like · the best part | spotlight
antes… ahora | before… now | "now" counts
no… sino | not… but | the *sino* part is true
la verdad es que | the truth is | honest verdict
sin embargo | however | formal *pero*
ya no | not any more | the habit stopped
a pesar de todo · menos mal que | despite it all · luckily | 🩹 consolation
a mí sí · a mí no | I do · I don't | 👤 the opposite person
:::

:::tip *Pero* is the king
More than 1 in 3 Edelsa T3 stories has a *pero* (my count: 20 of 56). Hear it, pen up.
:::

:::tip Likely topics
A wedding, a party, a show (official). Trips, first flight, nightlife, weather, shopping (Edelsa). Learn 10 words for each.
:::
