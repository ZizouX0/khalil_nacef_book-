## Tarea 1 · A1 · Una conversación, cinco huecos

A man and a woman chat about daily life. Fill **5 sentences** from **15 options** (a)–(ñ).
Slow speech (~130 words a minute), with pauses. The facts are said clearly (Guía p.47).
All 15 options are said in the audio: 10 are about something else.

:::flow Tarea 1 · the clock
30 s | read sentences + options
Play 1 | whole conversation
10 s | fill the sure ones
Play 2 | whole conversation again
30 s | finish and check
:::

:::steps Your routine
1. **Sort** the 15 options into 5 families of 3: *abuela / hermana / madre*, *primer / segundo / tercer*… (Guía p.75)
2. **Read the gap's grammar:** *en el ___ piso* needs *primer / segundo / tercer*.
3. **Label** each sentence with its family: 3 candidates left.
4. **Play 1:** pick the sure ones. **Play 2:** complete.
:::

:::trap 👤 The Stranger — about whom?
*Sara dice que el amigo de Mateo es ___*: Sara says *tímido*, Mateo adds *simpático*. Only *tímido* counts (Manual p.46).
**Fix:** ask "who says it, who does it?"
:::

:::trap ↩️ The U-turn — the last word wins
*«a las diez… no, perdona, a las diez y media»* → half past ten.
*«¿Vas en coche? — No, siempre voy en autobús.»* → the **reply** is the answer.
:::

:::remember
Find the family of 3.
Then catch the right sibling.
:::

## Tarea 2 · A2 · Cinco anuncios de radio

5 short ads or news items, one question each: a, b or c.
**The instruction never changes** (Guía p.49). 2 questions ask the general idea, 3 ask a detail.

:::flow Tarea 2 · the clock
30 s | read the 5 questions
Ad 1 · play 1 | listen for question 1 only
5 s | pause
Ad 1 · play 2 | check the detail
10 s | «Conteste a la pregunta número 1» → click
Ads 2–5 | same loop, then 30 s check
:::

:::steps Your routine
1. **In the 30 s, predict** each question's kind: ¿Qué anuncia? ¿A quién? ¿Dónde? ¿Cuándo? ¿Cuánto cuesta? (Manual p.50)
2. **Play 1: one ad, one question.** Listen only for it.
3. **Play 2: check** the number + its noun + the verb.
4. **Click and forget.** Later ads won't help.
:::

:::trap 🔁 The Echo — background scenery
The book ad mentions Egypt, Venice and cinema… but it sells **a novel** (Guía p.169).
**Fix:** ask "what is it **selling**?" The answer wears a disguise: *novela* → *libro*.
:::

:::trap 👻 The Ghost — the opposite
*«abierta hasta el martes»* ≠ *se abre el martes*. *«muebles clásicos»* rules out *modernos* (Guía p.169).
**Fix:** listen for *hasta / a partir de*, *no, sin*.
:::

:::remember
One ad, one question.
Click it, forget it.
:::

## ⭐ Tarea 3 · B1 · Ocho personas, un tema

**Your priority:** 8 points, the biggest block at your level.

- **8 people** (4 men, 4 women) tell a short personal story on **one topic**, 50–70 words each (Manual p.52).
- **11 sentences** (a)–(k). 8 are used. **3 match nobody.**
- They test the **main point**, said clearly. No hidden meanings.
- Likely topics: a wedding, a party, a show; trips, a first flight, nightlife, weather, shopping.

:::icons 👂 What the real audio sounds like (measured on 7 Edelsa exams)
⏱️ | ~25 s per person (~60 words), ~150 words a minute: near natural speed.
🖥️ | No "Persona 1" is announced. The screen shows the number.
🔇 | No beep, no "listen again". After the silence the audio just starts again.
👫 | Men and women often don't take turns. Follow the number, not the voice.
:::

:::flow Tarea 3 · the clock (about 10 min)
30 s | silence: read the 11 sentences, tag them, bracket the twins
Play 1 · ~25 s | eyes off the screen: ¿Quién? ¿Pero…? ¿Qué tal?
5 s | silence: compare your 2 candidates
Play 2 | the same recording: listen only for the difference
10 s | silence: click, cross the letter out, let it go
Personas 2–8 | start at once · the same loop × 7
Last 30 s | 8 filled, no letter twice
:::

:::steps Your routine
1. **30 s · Tag** each sentence in 1–2 words (*a-boat, b-countries…*). **Bracket the twins:** *Llegó con retraso* / *El espectáculo empezó tarde*.
2. **Play 1 · Listen, don't read.** ¿Quién? (who speaks) · ¿Pero…? (the turn) · ¿Qué tal? (the verdict). Write 3 key words + 1–2 candidate letters.
3. **Play 2 · The difference only.** Who? Did it really happen? That day or later?
4. **10 s · Click, cross out, move on.** Later people won't help (Guía p.76). Missed one? Guess from the letters left.
:::

:::big
Don't match words.
Match the story.
:::

### 🚪 Check the GUESTS

:::trap 👻 G · The Ghost — said, never happened
*«el grupo de música que habíamos contratado ==no fue==»* → ✗ *Hubo una orquesta* (Guía p.170)
**Fix:** hunt for *no, ni, sin, iba a, pensaba que*. Did it **really** happen?
:::

:::trap ↩️ U · The U-turn — after *pero*
*«Lo pasamos bien, sí, ==pero lo mejor fue== el viaje… por el Caribe»* → ✓ *Disfrutó de la luna de miel* (Guía p.171)
**Fix:** never click on sentence 1. More than 1 in 3 Edelsa stories has a *pero*: hear it, pen up.
:::

:::trap 🔁 E · The Echo — same word, other story
*«salí de casa ==un poco tarde==… me encontré una fiesta»* → ✓ *La fiesta se celebró en su lugar de trabajo*, ✗ *No llegó a tiempo* (Manual p.54)
**Fix:** the right answer wears a disguise: *llevábamos un año casados* → *aniversario de boda*.
:::

:::trap 🩹 S · The Silver lining — a happy last line
*«…y no por un buen motivo… A pesar de todo, me alegro»* → ✓ *Resultó un desastre* (Guía p.170)
**Fix:** judge the day or the trip, not life in general.
:::

:::trap 👯 T · The Twins — two sentences look alike
*Llegó con retraso* / *El espectáculo empezó tarde* (Manual p.55)
**Fix:** on play 2 ask **who**, **what** exactly, **when**.
:::

:::trap 👤 S · The Stranger — someone else's story
*«==Mi primo== llegó tarde»* → ✗ *El novio no llegó puntual*. *«A él le encantó; ==a mí no tanto==»* → the speaker did **not** love it (Guía p.170).
**Fix:** check every *él, ella, le, mi hermana*. The screen is about **the speaker**.
:::

:::remember
Name the GUEST behind every wrong letter.
Then click the story that is left.
:::

## 💬 Words that point to the answer

:::phrases Hear one? Pen up.
pero | but | ↩️ the point comes next
aunque | although | the other half counts
en realidad | actually | corrects the expectation
al final | in the end | often the answer
lo mejor fue · lo que sí | the best part · what I did like | spotlight
antes… ahora | before… now | "now" counts
no… sino | not… but | the *sino* part is true
sin embargo | however | formal *pero*
a pesar de todo · menos mal que | despite it all · luckily | 🩹 consolation
a mí sí · a mí no | I do · I don't | 👤 the opposite person
iba a · pensaba que | was going to · I thought | 👻 did it happen?
hasta el martes · a partir del martes | until · from Tuesday | T2: closes vs opens
gratis · sin pagar · te regalamos | free | T2: the "free" option
a las diecisiete cuarenta · ocho menos cuarto | 17:40 · 7:45 | T1–T2 times
:::
