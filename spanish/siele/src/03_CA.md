# 🎧 Comprensión auditiva (Listening)

## In 30 seconds

- **6 tasks, 38 questions, 55 minutes**, from A1 (Tarea 1) up to C1 (Tarea 6).
  - Every audio plays **twice, automatically**. You can't pause it, and you **can't go back** to a task (Manual p.43).
- **Your practice score of 18/38 is the very bottom of the B1 band (18–26).**
  - 17 would be A2 (Manual p.69; Edelsa p.139).
  - Goal: **22+, a safe B1.** Secure Tareas 1–3 (18 items: aim for 15–16), then guess everything else.
  - Blind guessing in T4–T6 gives about 7 points on average (my arithmetic, not an official figure). 15 + 7 = 22.
- **Tarea 3 is where your extra points are.** It has 8 items, the biggest block at your level. Read its section and the ear-training plan first.
- **Wrong answers lose you nothing.** Answer all 38 questions (Manual p.43; Guía p.78).
- **The reading time is your weapon.** You get 30 s before T1–T3 (Guía p.79).
- **The word you hear is often the trap.** The right option says the same idea **in other words** (Guía p.79).
- **Answers follow the order of the audio.** Never press *Siguiente* early: spare time is lost (Guía pp.78–79).

## How the test works

| Tarea | Nivel | What you get | Items | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A1 | 1 informal conversation, man + woman (150–170 words, < 2 min) | 5 | Complete 5 sentences from a bank of 15 options |
| 2 | A2 | 5 short radio ads or news items (≈ 50–65 words each) | 5 | Multiple choice, 3 options each |
| 3 | B1 | 8 short monologues on one topic (50–70 words each) | 8 | Match each person to 1 of 11 sentences (3 left over) |
| 4 | B2 | 1 radio/TV interview (550–600 words, < 5 min) | 8 | Multiple choice, 3 options |
| 5 | B2–C1 | 1 lecture in 6 fragments (450–500 words, < 4 min) | 6 | 1 of 3 ideas per fragment |
| 6 | C1 | 1 lecture (450–500 words, < 4 min) | 6 | Choose the 6 ideas mentioned, out of 12 |

Sources: Manual p.43; Guía pp.45–69. The Guía gives T2's length as 225–275, 250–300 *or* 50–65 words per text (pp.43, 48–49). The Manual says 250–275 (p.43), and I follow the Manual.

**Timing inside each task** (Guía pp.45, 48, 53, 58, 63, 68)

| 3 | 30 s | person 1 → 5 s → person 1 again → person 2… | 10 s after each person + 30 s at the end |

**Interface and rules**

- **Before you start:** there's a sound check (click *No* if it fails, and staff will help). The instructions are written on screen *and* read aloud (Guía pp.44, 78).
- **One screen per task.** *Siguiente* = no way back. The task closes by itself 30 s after the second play (Manual p.43; Guía p.73).
- **Blank items:** if you leave one, a warning appears. *Sí* = leave it, *No* = stay (Guía pp.73–74).
- **Use the mouse, not the arrow keys.** The keyboard can change your answers (Manual p.43).
- **Notes:** you can take notes while listening (Manual pp.45, 50). Edelsa says the centre gives you two blank sheets and a pen (Edelsa p.143).

**Score → level** (Manual p.69; Edelsa p.139)

| Correct (out of 38) | 5–9 | 10–17 | 18–26 | 27–32 | 33–38 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 | C1 |

**Accents** (Guía pp.43–44, 46, 59, 168)

- Every exam uses **at least 3 of the 6 big varieties** of Spanish.
- **T1–T3** are neutral ("internacional"), slow and very clear, but speakers keep their own pronunciation.
- **T4–T6** are at normal speed, and the Spanish can come from any country.
- **Modelo 0's voices:** T1 a Spanish man and a Venezuelan woman; T2 and T3 Spanish; T4 Mexican; T5 Argentinian; T6 Spanish.
- **Latin American words** like *mamá*, *sala* and *computadora* already appear at A1 (Guía p.168; Manual p.45).

## Task by task

### Before every task: the 30-second PREDICT routine

1. **Who and what?** Read the changing part of the instruction (names, topic) and think of 5 words you expect to hear (Manual pp.45, 53, 57).
2. **What is asked?** Underline the key word in each item: person, place, time, price, feeling.
3. **Group the options.**
   - T1: sort the 15 options into 5 families of 3 (Guía p.75).
   - T2: circle what is *different* between a, b and c.
   - T3: tag all 11 sentences (see Tarea 3).
4. **Think of synonyms.** For each option, think of another way to say it: *gratis* → *sin pagar, no cuesta nada, te regalamos*.
5. **While listening:**
   - Play 1: answer the easy items and put "?" next to the doubtful ones.
   - The 10 s between plays: pick which items to focus on (Guía p.79).
   - Play 2: confirm your answers.
   - Last 30 s: check that no item is blank.

### How the right answer hides: 9 patterns from the official samples

| # | Pattern | Official example | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paraphrase** | "le invita a una cena con este conocido cantante" → *conocer a un cantante* (Guía pp.163, 169) | Match the meaning, not the word |
| 2 | **Echo trap** | The same ad says *disco* and *concierto*, and both options are wrong (Guía p.169) | If you hear the exact word from an option, check again |
| 3 | **Question → "No"** | "¿la casa tiene piscina? — No." (Guía p.168); "¿Vas en coche? — No, siempre voy en autobús" (Manual p.46) | The **reply** is the answer |
| 4 | **Who does what** | "mis hijos juegan… mi mamá y yo leemos" (Guía p.168) | Check who the sentence is about |
| 5 | **Two of a kind** | *primer* piso = bathroom, *segundo* = bedrooms (Guía p.168) | Link each number to its noun |
| 6 | **Details → category** | "entre diecisiete y veinte años" → *para jóvenes* (Guía p.169) | Turn the details into a general label |
| 7 | **The turn after *pero*** | "Lo pasamos bien, sí, pero lo mejor fue el viaje…" (Guía p.171) | What follows *pero* is usually tested |
| 8 | **Negated event / opposite** | "muebles clásicos" rules out *modernos* (Guía p.169) | Listen for *no, ni, sin* and opposites |
| 9 | **Bent numbers** | "más de dos mil personas, la mayoría jóvenes" ≠ *dos mil jóvenes*; "abierta hasta el martes" ≠ *se abre el martes* (Guía p.169) | Check the number + its noun + the verb |

### Tarea 1 · A1 · Conversación + 5 huecos

- **Format:**
  - One informal man–woman conversation on everyday topics (Manual p.44; Guía p.46).
  - You complete 5 sentences from a shared bank of 15 options, (a)–(ñ), in a *--Elija--* dropdown (Manual p.44).
  - Only the names and topic change in the instruction (Guía p.46).
- **What they test:** facts that are said clearly. **No inference needed** (Guía p.47).
- **Patterns that repeat:**
  - **5 families of 3 look-alike options**, scattered through the bank (Guía pp.47, 75). Modelo 0 had *abuela/hermana/madre*, *primer/segundo/tercer*, *leen/juegan/cantan*, *terraza/piscina/balcón* and *cinco/diez/quince* (Guía p.162).
  - **The 10 wrong options are also said in the audio**, but about something else (Guía p.47).
  - **Vocabulary fields:** numbers, times, days, months, rooms, places in town, transport, leisure, shops, food, restaurants, prices, jobs, family (Guía p.75).
  - **Grammar:** present tense, *hay*, *gustar*, *poder/querer/pensar* + infinitive (Guía p.75).
- **Step-by-step strategy:**
  1. Decide the word type each gap needs. *"de la ___"* needs a feminine noun. *"en el ___ piso"* needs *primer/segundo/tercer*.
  2. Write the option family next to each sentence.
  3. Play 1: select what you're sure of.
  4. Play 2: complete and check (Manual p.47).
- **Traps:**
  - Patterns 3–5 in the table above.
  - **Check who the sentence quotes.** In *"Sara dice que el amigo de Mateo es…"*, Sara says *tímido* and Mateo adds *simpático*, so only *tímido* counts (Manual p.46).
  - **The last decision wins.** A customer rejects the first book ("Bueno, no me gusta mucho… ¿Hay otros títulos?") and buys the second (Edelsa Transcripciones, Examen 5, Tarea 1).
- **Practise in the books:** Manual pp.44–47; Guía p.162 (transcript p.168, solutions p.183).

### Tarea 2 · A2 · Cinco anuncios o noticias de radio

- **Format:**
  - 5 ads or news items, each played twice. Then you hear *"Conteste a la pregunta número X"* and get 10 s (Guía p.48).
  - One question per ad, with 3 options.
  - **The instruction never changes** (Guía pp.49, 73).
- **What they test:** 2 items ask for the general idea (*"En este anuncio se hace publicidad de…"*). 3 items ask for details: who, where, when, how much (Guía pp.51, 75).
- **Patterns that repeat:**
  - **Topics:** shop and travel offers, leisure, contests, courses, renting accommodation, tourism, transport, exhibitions, concerts, cinema and theatre (Guía pp.49, 75; Manual p.48).
  - **The right answer is a paraphrase:** *precios baratos* → *precios más bajos*; *novela* → *libro* (Guía pp.163, 169).
  - **Wrong options** contradict the audio or say something it doesn't (Guía p.51).
- **Step-by-step strategy:**
  1. In the 30 s, ask the Manual's 5 questions for each ad: *¿Qué anuncia? ¿A quién se dirige? ¿Dónde es? ¿Cuándo es? ¿Cuánto cuesta?* (Manual p.50).
  2. While an ad plays, focus **only on its question** (Manual p.49).
  3. On play 2, check the audio gives the **exact** information (Manual p.51).
  4. Answer and move on. Later ads won't help (Guía p.75).
- **Traps:**
  - **Background details:** the book ad mentions Egypt, Venice and cinema, but it is selling a novel (Guía p.169).
  - **Times and days:** *"de cinco de la tarde a diez de la noche"* ≠ *por las mañanas*.
  - **Same word, other meaning:** *por cambio de exposición* ≠ *otra exposición* (Guía p.169).
- **Practise in the books:** Manual pp.48–51; Guía p.163 (transcript p.169, solutions p.183).

### Tarea 3 · B1 · Ocho personas, un tema (your priority)

#### How it works (confirmed from the sources)

| What | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 8 (Persona 1–8) = 8 points | Manual pp.43, 52 |
| Options | 11 short sentences (a)–(k) in a *--Elija--* dropdown. 8 are used, **3 match nobody** | Manual p.52; Guía p.57 |
| Audio | 8 monologues on **one topic** named in the instruction, 50–70 words each, the whole set under 5 min per listening | Guía pp.53–54; Manual p.43 |
| Speakers | 4 men + 4 women, alternating. In Modelo 0, though, Personas 7 *and* 8 are both men | Manual p.52; Guía p.171 |
| Plays | Each person **twice in a row**, with a 5 s gap | Guía p.53; Manual p.52 |
| Reading time | **30 s** before Persona 1 | Guía p.53 |
| Answer time | **10 s** after each person's second play; 30 s after Persona 8 | Guía p.53; transcript pp.170–171 |
| Total | About 10 min (my estimate: 30 s + 8 × ~70 s + 30 s) | (estimate) |
| Language | B1: past tenses, *pero/porque/aunque*, lots of pronouns and relative clauses. Slow, very clear, neutral Spanish | Guía pp.54, 76 |
| Instruction | *"Usted va a escuchar a ocho personas que hablan sobre [tema]. Elija la frase que corresponde a cada persona. Va a escuchar a cada persona dos veces. Ahora tiene 30 segundos para leer las frases."* | Manual p.52; Guía p.54 |

- **What they test:** the **main point** of a short story about a personal experience (Manual p.52; Guía pp.53, 76). The Guía says the texts can be "monólogos o conversaciones" (p.53). Every official and Edelsa sample is a monologue.
- **Topics seen so far:**
  - Official: a wedding day (Guía p.164), a party and a show (Manual pp.53, 55).
  - Edelsa: trips abroad, first plane trip, nightlife in your city, Spanish beaches, places to visit in Madrid, weather forecasts, how people shop (Edelsa Transcripciones, Examen 1–7, Tarea 3).
  - **Travel, going out and celebrations dominate.** The Guía's list also includes housing, services, sport, weather, culture, work and nature (Guía p.54).
- **Sentence types** (Guía p.164; Manual pp.53, 55). Tag each sentence by its type:
  - **Event:** *Llovió mucho. Llegó con retraso. Perdió algo.*
  - **Verdict:** *Fue un éxito. Resultó un desastre. Se aburrió mucho.*
  - **People:** *Conoció a alguien. Fue con uno de sus amigos. Discutió con alguien.*
  - **Place / occasion:** *Se celebró en un barco. Era su aniversario de boda.*
- **How Edelsa differs from the official format:**
  - Edelsa's instruction says *"Elija la oración…"* and *"Tiene 30 segundos para leer las oraciones"*. The official one says *"Elija la frase…"* and *"Ahora tiene 30 segundos para leer las frases"*. The meaning is the same.
  - Edelsa's transcripts don't mark the speaker's sex or the [5 s]/[10 s] pauses. Modelo 0's do (Guía pp.170–171).
  - Edelsa's lengths match the official ones: 49–72 words, mean ≈ 60 (my count of 56 monologues).
  - **But** Edelsa Examen 4–6 are descriptions and forecasts in the present and future (beaches, restaurants, weather), not past-tense anecdotes. Use them as extra-hard detail practice. Exams 1, 2, 3 and 7 are closer to the real thing.

#### Your routine: reading time, play 1, play 2

**Reading time (30 s)**

1. Note the topic (2 s).
2. Read the 11 sentences and give each a **1–2-word English tag** on your paper: "a-boat, b-countries, c-countryside…" (20 s).
3. **Bracket the twins**: sentences that look alike, such as *Llegó con retraso* / *El espectáculo empezó tarde* (8 s).
4. Write a column "P1 __ P2 __ … P8 __".

**Play 1 (each person). Listen for three things:**

- **WHO** is speaking: man or woman, and their role (bride, guest, parent…).
- **The TURN word** (*pero, en realidad, al final, lo mejor fue, lo que sí, aunque*). The sentence after it is gold.
- **The VERDICT**: good, bad, surprise, change.

Keep your eyes off the options while the person speaks. Write 3 key words ("rain after church"), then 1–2 candidate letters.

**5-second gap:** look at the twin of each of your candidates.

**Play 2.** Listen **only for the difference between your candidates**:

- Who did it?
- Did it happen, or was it only expected?
- When was it: that day, or years later?

Then check that the rejected sentences match only **isolated words** in the audio, not its meaning (Manual p.54).

**10 seconds.** Click your choice, write the letter on paper and cross it out of the list. Undecided? Choose the one that matches the speaker's turn or verdict, and **move on**. Later people won't help (Guía p.76).

**Final 30 s.** Check:

- all 8 dropdowns are filled;
- no letter is used twice;
- the 3 left-over sentences feel "never really said".

If you missed a person, eliminating the letters already used leaves you only 3–4 to choose from.

#### The trap catalogue (from the Manual, Modelo 0 and Edelsa)

1. **The opening isn't the point.**
   - "Yo me lo pasé muy bien." The answer turns out to be *Llovió mucho*, not *Fue un éxito* (Modelo 0, Persona 1, Guía pp.170, 183).
   - Another speaker opens with a night spent sleeping in an airport, but the story is about the speaker's first flight as a baby (Edelsa Transcripciones, Examen 2, Tarea 3, Persona 3).
   - **Defence:** wait for the whole story (Manual p.54).
2. **The turn: *pero / lo que sí / lo mejor fue*.**
   - "La mañana estaba despejada pero, cuando salimos de la iglesia, de repente se nubló y empezó a llover muy fuerte" (Guía p.170).
   - "Lo pasamos bien, sí, pero lo mejor fue el viaje que hicimos luego por el Caribe" → *Disfrutó de la luna de miel* (Guía p.171).
   - "La ciudad no me gustó mucho… Lo que sí me gustó fue el recorrido… con el barco por el Sena" (Edelsa Ex.1, T3, P1).
3. **Verdict first, consolation last.**
   - "Fue un día que nunca olvidaré, y no por un buen motivo." The speaker ends with "A pesar de todo, me alegro de haberme casado…" → *Resultó un desastre*, not *Fue un éxito* (Guía p.170).
   - **Defence:** the verdict is about the *topic* (the day), not life in general.
4. **A scare with a happy ending ≠ a disaster.**
   - "Llegamos muy tarde… Menos mal que todo quedó en un susto. Lo demás salió fenomenal" → *El novio no llegó puntual* (Guía p.171).
   - "Al final no pasó nada, pero pensé que acabaríamos como esos supervivientes" (Edelsa Ex.2, T3, P8): fear, not an accident.
5. **Wrong person.**
   - "Mi primo llegó tarde y se le olvidaron los anillos" is **not** *El novio no llegó puntual* (Guía p.170).
   - "En cambio, a mi hermana le gusta ir sola": the sister's habit isn't the speaker's (Edelsa Ex.7, T3, P7).
   - **Defence:** check every *él / ella / le / mi primo*.
6. **The speaker's role is implied, never stated.**
   - A woman: "En cuanto comenzó la misa, empecé a llorar" = *La novia lloró mucho*.
   - A man whose car broke down while "Ana con cara de preocupación" waited = the groom (Guía p.171).
   - **Defence:** word endings help you here: *emocionada, contento, española* (Edelsa Ex.2, P4 and P7; Ex.1, P7).
7. **Negated or cancelled event.**
   - "el grupo de música que habíamos contratado para el baile no fue" rules out *Hubo una orquesta* (Guía p.170).
   - "Nosotros también queríamos una foto, pero la azafata no nos dejó levantarnos" (Edelsa Ex.2, P6): they never got the photo.
8. **Lists and decoration.**
   - "las flores, la música, las fotos, el baile, el viaje…" lists music and a trip, but the point is "Todo salió perfecto" → *Fue un éxito* (Guía p.170).
   - Numbers are usually decoration too: "Tengo ya 87 años" (Edelsa Ex.2, P7).
9. **Same word, different story.**
   - "era mi último día y salí de casa un poco tarde. Cuando llegué a mi despacho, me encontré una fiesta que no me esperaba" → *La fiesta se celebró en su lugar de trabajo*, not *No llegó a tiempo* (Manual p.54).
   - "Iba a alguna boda solo por compromiso" → *Antes no soportaba las fiestas*, not a wedding statement (Manual p.54).
10. **Paraphrase and category.** None of these share the key word with the sentence on screen:
    - "reunión de exalumnos de mi escuela" → *Había gente que había conocido en el colegio*
    - "la guardería a la que va mi hijo" → *una fiesta para niños*
    - "llevábamos un año casados" → *aniversario de boda* (Manual p.54)
    - "en lo alto de un cerro… entre los árboles" → *Tuvo lugar en el campo* (Guía p.170)
11. **Expectation vs reality; before vs now.**
    - "para nosotros la boda era solo un compromiso. Pero lo que no esperaba era un día lleno de sentimientos" (Guía p.171).
    - "A mí me asustaba entrar en un aeropuerto… Pero en realidad no fue tan complicado" (Edelsa Ex.2, P1).
    - "Antes no estaba acostumbrado a esta vida, pero ahora siempre disfruto de la noche" (Edelsa Ex.3, P7).
12. **A concession with *aunque*: the exception is the point.**
    - "Aunque mis metas preferidas suelen ser lugares cálidos, las vacaciones que recuerdo con mayor placer fueron en Finlandia" (Edelsa Ex.1, P5).
    - "Aunque mi mujer es mexicana, nos casamos en Madrid" → guests from many countries (Guía p.171).
13. **Twin sentences on screen.**
    - *Llegó con retraso* / *El espectáculo empezó tarde*; *La entrada fue muy cara* / *Las entradas se habían acabado* (Manual p.55).
    - *Tuvo lugar en el campo* / *Fue en su pueblo* / *Se celebró en un barco* (Guía p.164).
    - *Se casaba un familiar* / *Fue una reunión familiar* / *Era su aniversario de boda* (Manual p.53).
    - **Defence:** ask *who* or *what* exactly.
14. **Two speakers, the same idea with opposite meaning.**
    - "solo compro lo que necesito" vs "compro cosas que no necesito… porque pierdo el control" (Edelsa Ex.7, P2/P3).
    - "recibe muchos turistas" vs "sin demasiados turistas" (Edelsa Ex.4, P3/P4).
    - Words shared across monologues are deliberate (Manual p.53; Guía p.57).
15. **The praised thing isn't the speaker's own.**
    - A speaker from a dull village near Sevilla praises Valencia's nightlife (Edelsa Ex.3, P4).
    - "Más que por su historia…, la ciudad… atrapa por la cantidad de gente en sus calles": the reason after *más que* is the point (Edelsa Ex.3, P8).

### Tareas 4–6 · B2–C1 · Survival kit

- **T4 (interview, 8 questions):**
  - Each item usually matches one of the interviewer's questions, so use them as signposts (Guía p.76).
  - The answer is often the opinion after *pero yo creo…*. Modelo 0: everyone says money is the first problem, "pero yo creo que están confundidos" → the answer is a good plan (Guía p.172).
- **T5 (6 fragments, 1 of 3 ideas each):** follow the fragment number. The tested idea can be a secondary one (Guía pp.63, 77).
- **T6 (choose 6 of 12):** the options follow the order of the talk, and the second play matters most (Guía p.77). **Always select exactly six** (Manual p.64).
- **Easy points:** rule out absurd or extreme options, and never leave a blank (Guía p.78; Edelsa p.139).

## Top tips & tricks

1. **Know the instructions by heart.** Only the people and topic change, and T2's never changes (Guía p.73; Manual pp.44, 48, 52).
2. **Use the reading time to predict** (Guía pp.45, 79; Manual p.43).
3. **Items follow the order of the audio.** A general question may come first or last (Guía p.79; Manual p.129).
4. **Wrong options are mentioned in the audio.** Pick the option that says the same idea in different words (Guía p.79; Manual pp.45, 129).
5. **Don't panic on the first play.** The second play is for confirming (Manual pp.43, 45).
6. **Use the 10 s between plays** to choose which items to focus on (Guía p.79).
7. **T2 and T3: answer straight after each audio.** Later audios won't help (Guía pp.75–76).
8. **T3: never choose on a single word.** Wait for the whole story (Manual pp.53–55).

### How to train your ear in 7 days

**Materials**

- Manual T3 audio and transcripts (Extensión digital). The Manual advises listening *before* reading the transcripts (Manual p.43).
- Modelo 0 transcripts (Guía pp.168–171). There is no audio in the Guía.
- **Edelsa Transcripciones, Examen 1–7, Tarea 3.** Edelsa's audio is *pista 3* of each exam (Edelsa p.144).
- **No audio?** Use a text-to-speech (TTS) app: your phone's read-aloud function, or any TTS with a Spanish voice and a speed setting. Use a Spain voice *and* a Mexican or Argentinian voice, because the exam mixes varieties.

**Four techniques**

1. **Listen → read → listen (L-R-L), 15 min.**
   - Listen to all 8 people twice *without the text*, and write each person's story in 3 words.
   - Then read the transcript. Circle the turn word (*pero, al final…*) and underline the decoration (lists, numbers, other people).
   - Listen again without the text: you should now hear every circle.
2. **Shadowing, 5 min.** Play one monologue sentence by sentence and repeat it aloud half a second behind the speaker, copying the rhythm. This trains you to hear word boundaries at speed.
3. **Number dictation, 5 min.** Write every time, date, price and age you hear. Use Ejercicio 5, Modelo 0 T2 (Guía p.169) read by TTS, or 8 numbers you type into TTS yourself.
4. **Speed ladder.** TTS at **0.8×** on days 1–3, **1.0×** on days 4–7. Finish with real recorded voices (the Manual audio) on day 6, because TTS isn't the real thing.
