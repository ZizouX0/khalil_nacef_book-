:::remember
Yellow note = the ONE thing to remember.

:::golden The 3 rules for the whole exam
1. **Never leave an answer blank.** In reading and listening, wrong answers lose you nothing.
2. **Answer every point you are asked.** In writing and speaking, "task completion" is marked on its own.
3. **Keep going when it gets hard.** The tasks go from A1 to C1, so the later ones are *meant* to be difficult.
:::
